## Context

The constitution says a law outranks an ADR and that changing a law takes an ADR that updates the law page in the same
commit ([constitution](/constitution/index), "Amending a law"). Since the laws were written, Accepted ADRs and locked owner
decisions changed what several laws describe, and the pages were not updated. The gap questionnaire of 2026-10-19 lists the
conflicts (B5, B7, B9, B19, B21). As written, the laws would make those Accepted ADRs "wrong". This ADR records the
amendments. Each follows from an existing Accepted ADR or a locked owner decision; nothing new is decided.

## Decision

The law pages change as follows, each with a History entry pointing here.

1. **Boundaries.** Shared packages expose decorators, base classes and typed config contracts, not `define*()` factories
   ([0094](/adr/0094-decorators-only-authoring)); the principle is renamed "shared packages hold mechanisms, apps hold
   definitions". Cross-service types live in a package's `./contracts` entry or in `@stackra/contracts`, not in separate
   contract packages ([0117](/adr/0117-contracts-placement-and-decorator-ownership), which superseded 0015).
2. **HTTP and events.**
   - Every operation is one action exposed by an adapter; gen emits the `api()` ([0098](/adr/0098-actions-are-the-unit),
     [0091](/adr/0091-generated-encore-app-in-stackra-folder)).
   - Boundary validation is `@stackra/validation` compiled to Zod, not Encore's `Min`/`MaxLen`
     ([0095](/adr/0095-zod-inside-field-decorators)).
   - Domain events stay in-process; integration events are named `<aggregate>.<verb>.v<n>` (`user.created.v1`,
     [0078](/adr/0078-messaging-layer-eight-packages)). Whether a service prefix is added stays open (gap C27).
   - A subscriber declares `@OnEvent` and imports only the producer's `./contracts`; it never imports another service's
     `Topic` (0078, 0091).
   - The envelope's `actor` is the 0098 actor model with its `onBehalfOf` chain; `PublicId` and the prefix registry live in
     `@stackra/contracts` (0117).
3. **Tenancy and security.** The ltree scope tree leaves the base law and becomes a section that binds only when the Pro
   scope driver (`@stackra-pro/scope`) is installed; free `@stackra/iam` resolves RBAC with no scope dependency
   ([0088](/adr/0088-tenancy-free-pool-pro-silo-and-scope) decisions 5 and 2, owner decisions S2, S3 and S12, locked
   2026-10-19). The effective-permission formula marks its scope step as Pro.
4. **Security SEC3.** The audit record is written by the action pipeline's audit step and carries the full `Actor` chain, so
   an agent action names the agent and the human it acted for (0098 decisions 3, 6 and 7).
5. **Delegation D4.** The lead pushes, opens pull requests and merges only through the merge gate; the owner merges the
   version pull request and promotes to production ([0119](/adr/0119-merge-policy-and-release-authority)). The brief names
   specs as `.agents/specs/sta-<n>-<slug>` ([0120](/adr/0120-sdlc-spec-location-lanes-and-change-risk)). The preamble already
   follows [0121](/adr/0121-sdlc-tooling-roles-and-linear) D10.
6. **Index.** The dependency-direction principle follows [0003](/adr/0003-tier-dependency-direction): `config` → `lib` →
   `app` and `service`, with apps and services as peers.
7. **Rules and standards that restate these laws follow them**: `.agents/rules/tenancy.md`, `standards/packages`,
   `standards/i18n-rtl`. The `.specify` constitution is regenerated by `pnpm agents:sync`.

Not amended here, because each needs an owner choice:

- **B20:** whether `defineToken` ([0005](/adr/0005-di-container-and-legacy-decorators) decision 2) is carved out of 0094 or
  renamed `createToken`.
- **SEC1 and the HTTP auth flag** against Proposed [0093](/adr/0093-better-auth-integration)
  (`auth: none|optional|required`), and the public-id examples against Proposed
  [0082](/adr/0082-public-ids-and-api-areas) (DA9): both wait for those ADRs (OR1).
- **SEC3 versus Proposed [0046](/adr/0046-global-data-invariants-and-per-model-opt-ins)** (audit as a per-model opt-in) beyond
  the pipeline wording above.

## Consequences

- The laws no longer contradict Accepted ADRs on authoring style, events, validation, tenancy, audit or delegation.
- The constitution now names Pro packages, so a free-only product reads the scope sections as not applying to it.
- Agents that read `.specify/memory/constitution.md` get the amended text after the sync.

## Enforcement

`pnpm agents:check` (in `pnpm validate`) fails when the generated `.specify` constitution drifts from the law pages. The
laws' own Enforcement lines are unchanged; their checks stay planned where they were.
