## Context

Wave 10 built a Stripe Terminal driver of `TerminalGateway` on the Stripe Connection: server-driven readers with
on-reader tipping and cancel-then-reconcile. The owner answered that own-account payments in the US start with Stripe,
and that Square and Adyen are built when the first design partner needs one (RV-53). Tap to Pay on iPhone and Android
is a launch item in the US and for Academorix (US-36, RV-45) and needs an Apple entitlement per app bundle (R3-SO-12).

## Decision

1. **The split for this family.** The provider's cloud drives the reader. The `TerminalGateway` driver is the
   provider's Connection on the server, reached through `PaymentGateways`; `CardTerminal` in `@stackra/peripherals`
   only records which reader a till uses and reports its state. The till never speaks the reader's protocol.
2. **Stripe Terminal.** Built. Server-driven readers, on-reader tipping and cancel-then-reconcile, for `us` and `eu`
   and wherever a pack offers Stripe. Stripe Terminal's Tap to Pay is a `SoftPos` driver of the same family in the
   Expo staff app, using Stripe's SDK as a native module.
3. **Square.** Driver `square` (gateway and Terminal) is built when the first design partner on Square hardware signs
   (RV-53, R4-5); until then a US partner on other hardware moves to certified Stripe readers or waits.
4. **Adyen.** Driver `adyen` (gateway and terminals through Adyen's cloud terminal API) under the same trigger as
   Square, for `us` and `eu`.
5. **Tap to Pay needs an entitlement per app.** Every app bundle that offers Tap to Pay, including each white-label
   app, holds the platform entitlement for it; `launch-gate:<pack>:<product>` lists it.
6. **Choice is data.** As in 0377 decision 8: `market-pack:<code>.payments.terminal-providers`, managed or own
   Connections, tips from settings and rule sets.
7. **Values.** Pack data: terminal providers per market, `payments.byo-providers` (`us`: Stripe, Square, Adyen,
   PayPal; `eu`: Stripe, Adyen) and the launch-gate rows. Settings and rule sets: tips. Code: the drivers.

## Consequences

- A US or EU merchant can bring its own Stripe account and readers at launch; Square and Adyen follow demand.
- White-label apps carry an extra store review step for the Tap to Pay entitlement.

## Enforcement

planned — each driver's conformance suite against the provider's test mode; the launch gate lists each certified
driver and the Tap to Pay entitlement per app bundle.
