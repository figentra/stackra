## Context

Three things were mixed up in the OS planning: who can sign in, who can act, and who a tenant does business with. The
OS draft had a `people` service owning a tenant-scoped Person (OS-351) and Company (OS-352), identity owning the login,
and devices in identity ([0235](/adr/0235-devices-and-iot-minimal-now-full-later) decision 1) while the Commerce answer
33 put them in a devices service (OS-43). Shoppers needed a sign-in that works across merchants without merchants
seeing each other's data (OS-25), and residency rules put sign-in identifiers inside each region (OS-22). The owner
answered in chat on 2026-10-25 (OS rounds 3, 5 and 7) and, in round 22, kept the principals and parties mechanism in
Stackra and Figentra's own choices (the Figentra ID name, the `parties` and `devices` services, sign-in methods,
support access terms) in Figentra OS ADRs ([0259](/adr/0259-module-placement-three-homes)).

## Decision

1. **Login holds no business record.** The login layer, on ZITADEL with one instance per region
   ([0353](/adr/0353-data-residency-a-region-fixed-at-sign-up-a-login-directory-that-only-routes-and-copies-within-a-jurisdiction)),
   holds credentials, sign-in methods and sessions. It holds no business record about a person.
2. **Principals live in identity and IAM.** Every actor that can authenticate or be granted access is a principal in
   identity and IAM: humans, AI agents, bots, service accounts, API keys, OAuth apps, devices and integrations. Grants,
   roles and approvals apply to principals only.
3. **Parties hold the tenant's records of people and organisations.** Per tenant, a parties model holds `Person` and
   `Organisation` records: customers, clients, students, leads, suppliers and companies. A party is not a principal.
4. **A party links to a login only by sign-up or consent.** A `Person` links to a login only when the human signs up,
   or consents to the link. A party without a login is normal.
5. **Products add their own fields.** Products extend parties with their own fields and records, never with a second
   person or organisation model.
6. **End customers sign in once.** An end customer has one login per region, shown in the product's brand. Each
   merchant holds its own customer profile (a party) linked to that login only by sign-up or consent. Merchants never
   see each other's data or each other's customer profiles.
7. **Devices: credentials in identity, two packages** (OS-43, owner round 22). Identity issues and revokes device
   credentials; a device is still a principal (decision 2). The device registry leaves identity (this replaces 0235
   decision 1). `@stackra/devices` holds device identity, the registry and pairing ports; `@stackra/peripherals` holds
   the peripheral ports: printers, scanners, cash drawers, scales, customer displays and card terminals. The two stay
   separate packages, and a devices service that implements the registry uses both.
8. **Till staff** (Commerce answers 31 and 32): PIN quick-switch and a manager override above limits live in Stackra
   identity and IAM. A PIN-only cashier is a principal; whether it counts as a seat is the product's choice.

## Consequences

- One question has one home: "can it sign in" is the login layer, "may it do this" is IAM, "who is this customer to
  us" is parties.
- A shopper with accounts at two merchants has one login and two separate profiles, so a merchant's export, erasure or
  consent touches only its own profile.
- Every product's customer, client or student model becomes a reference to a party plus its own fields; product
  migrations must map their own person tables onto parties.
- Revoking a device stays one revocation path in identity, while inventory and pairing move out of identity.

## Enforcement

planned — a catalogue check: no service other than the one owning parties declares a person or organisation model,
and no service other than identity issues credentials. planned — a conformance test in the identity service that a
revoked device credential is refused (as 0235 already plans). Nothing checks these rules yet.

## Open questions

- **Staff records.** The principals report lists a staff or employee record separate from the user account (I5).
  Whether staff are parties, workforce records, or both, is not decided.
- **Staff accounts per region.** [0206](/adr/0206-zitadel-is-the-identity-provider-and-stackra-identity-is-provider-neutral)
  decision 5 says Staff have one global account. With one ZITADEL instance per region, whether staff keep one account
  per region or a global one is not decided.
- **Customer realm.** [0206](/adr/0206-zitadel-is-the-identity-provider-and-stackra-identity-is-provider-neutral)
  decision 5 gave each tenant a ZITADEL org for its end customers. How the customer realm and per-tenant login
  branding map onto one regional directory is not designed yet.
