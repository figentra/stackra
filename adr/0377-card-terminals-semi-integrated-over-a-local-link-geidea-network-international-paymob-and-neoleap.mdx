## Context

Commerce launches with Geidea, NeoLeap or Surepay, Network International, Paymob and Stripe Terminal in its first
markets (COM-231). Wave 10 built the `CardTerminal` port in `@stackra/peripherals` (pair and report only, with a
`memory` driver), the `TerminalGateway` interface in `@stackra/payments` (present an amount, read or cancel the
session) with a Stripe Terminal driver, and `Capability.CardTerminal` in `@stackra/platform`. The Gulf and Egypt
providers' terminals are semi-integrated: the till sends the amount to the terminal over a local link (serial, USB,
Bluetooth or the shop's network) under the provider's electronic cash register (ECR) protocol, and the terminal talks
to its acquirer. A cloud API that a server can drive was not confirmed for any of them. 0339 decision 1 keeps the
terminal's protocol in the payments driver and leaves pairing and state to the peripheral port.

## Decision

1. **The split for this family.** The provider's ECR protocol is a `TerminalGateway` driver in a client entry of
   `@stackra/payments` (`./native` for the Expo staff app, and the Electron till's main process), one driver per
   provider. The local link is `@stackra/peripherals`: `CardTerminal` pairs the terminal and reports its state, and its
   native transport carries the protocol's bytes. Neither package knows the other's internals.
2. **The server learns the result from the till, then checks it.** The till reports the terminal's outcome to the
   payments service as a terminal session result; the service records it as pending confirmation and confirms it
   against the provider's settlement or transaction report where the provider offers one. A lost answer is handled
   by cancel-then-reconcile, as built for Stripe Terminal. An integrated card payment never completes offline (0339).
3. **Geidea.** Driver `geidea` on the provider's ECR protocol over the link the terminal model supports. The protocol
   specification and test terminals come from Geidea under its integration agreement; the driver is written against
   that specification, not inferred. Certification by Geidea is a row of `launch-gate:sa` and of every pack that
   offers it.
4. **Network International.** Driver `network-international` on N-Genius terminal ECR integration, under the same
   conditions as Geidea: specification and terminals from the provider, certification as a launch-gate row of each
   pack that offers it (`ae` first).
5. **Paymob.** Driver `paymob` for Paymob's terminals in `eg` and the Gulf markets where it offers them, under the same
   conditions. Paymob's online gateway driver is separate and unaffected.
6. **NeoLeap or Surepay.** One of the two is chosen for `sa` by the owner or sales (COM-231 names both). The chosen one
   gets a driver under the same conditions; the other is not built until a merchant needs it.
7. **SoftPOS from a Gulf provider.** Where a pack names a Gulf SoftPOS SDK (Geidea or Checkout.com, R3-SO-12), it is a
   further `TerminalGateway` driver on the staff app's phone, under the same certification rule.
8. **Choice is data.** Which terminal providers a market offers is `market-pack:<code>.payments.terminal-providers`.
   A merchant with its own acquiring contract connects its own provider account (own mode); a merchant on Figentra
   Pay uses the managed Connection ([0376](/adr/0376-provider-resolution-ports-credential-references-managed-or-own-accounts-and-the-telephony-ports)
   decision 5). The tip prompt and its suggested amounts are a rule set and settings, never driver constants.
9. **Values.** Pack data: terminal providers per market and the launch-gate certification rows. Settings: tip
   suggestions and whether to prompt. Rule sets: when to prompt for a tip. Seed data: the device catalogue's terminal
   models. Code: the protocol drivers only.

## Consequences

- Each driver waits on its provider's specification and test terminals; nothing is written from guesses.
- The till, not the server, holds the live terminal session, so the payments service must accept a reported result
  and confirm it later.
- Each driver adds a native module, which 0253 decision 3 already allows with this evidence.

## Enforcement

planned — each driver ships a conformance suite against the provider's test terminal or simulator, and each pack's
launch gate lists the certified driver. Nothing checks it yet.
