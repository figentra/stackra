## Context

`PackStore`, `fetchPacks` and `resolvePack` existed, but nothing handed a service its deployment's verified packs as
one injectable dependency, so identity, billing and notifications each wired the store, the hashes and the keys.

## Decision

1. **A port.** `ResolvedPackSource` (key `market_packs.ResolvedPackSource`) gives `packs()`, the deployment's verified
   pack versions, and `valueFor(market, key, options)`, the value in force on a day (today, UTC, by default) under the
   tenant's pins, statuses and sections. A driver supplies `packs()` only; `valueFor` is the port's, so every driver
   answers alike.
2. **Drivers.** `store` (default) fetches the hashes in `MarketPacksConfig.packHashes` from `PackStore` and verifies
   them against `MarketPacksConfig.trustedKeys` once per process; a failed load is retried on the next call. `memory`
   serves packs handed to it. The OS loader (STA-763) is a further driver or a host `@Replaces`.
3. **Binding.** `MarketPacksModule` (the `./node` entry) exports the port; gen binds the driver from config `source`
   (`STACKRA_MARKET_PACKS_SOURCE`). Which packs a deployment trusts is deploy-time configuration, never per tenant.

## Consequences

- `@stackra/market-packs` depends on `@stackra/config` and `@stackra/validation` at runtime (the lockfile gains those
  links).
- New pack versions reach a running service with a new deployment config.
