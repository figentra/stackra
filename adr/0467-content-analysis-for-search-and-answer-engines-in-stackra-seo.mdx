## Context

[0301](/adr/0301-stackra-seo-package) gives `@stackra/seo` sitemaps, robots rules, structured data, canonical URLs and
alternates, but no analysis of the content itself. Merchants and editors need what Yoast gives WordPress and what Shopify's
search-listing panel gives products:

- a live search-result preview;
- length limits measured the way the engines measure them;
- keyphrase and readability checks with a traffic-light list.

They also need the same for answer engines, the AI assistants that quote pages. Arabic is a first-class language, and
English readability formulas don't apply to it.

## Decision

1. **The engine lives in `@stackra/seo`, as pure functions** (`core` entry), so it runs on the server, in a Worker and in the
   editor. Its input is a content snapshot: title, meta description, slug, focus keyphrase(s), body as the builder document
   JSON or plain text, images with alt text, links, locale, and content type. Its output is a list of
   `{ id, group: "seo" | "readability" | "aeo", rating: "good" | "ok" | "bad", messageKey, values }` and a score per group.
   Messages are i18n keys.
2. **SEO checks:**
   - keyphrase in the title, introduction, slug, meta description, subheadings and image alt;
   - keyphrase density, and keyphrase used before on another page (through a port the app implements);
   - title width in pixels against the engine's limit, and meta description length;
   - text length per content type;
   - internal and outbound links;
   - a single H1 and heading order.
3. **Readability checks, one rule set per language:**
   - English: sentence and paragraph length, passive voice, transition words, consecutive sentence starts, a Flesch score.
   - Arabic: sentence and paragraph length, transition words (لذلك، علاوة على ذلك، …), and diacritics-insensitive keyphrase
     matching with alef/ya/ta-marbuta normalisation from `@stackra/i18n`. No Flesch score, because it is not valid for Arabic.
4. **AEO checks and outputs:**
   - an answer-first summary of 40-60 words near the top;
   - question-shaped subheadings;
   - FAQ, HowTo, Product and Article structured data, generated through the existing escaper (0301 decision 4);
   - entity links (`sameAs`);
   - author and updated date present;
   - `llms.txt` and `llms-full.txt` generated beside the sitemaps;
   - robots groups for AI crawlers (GPTBot, ClaudeBot, PerplexityBot, Google-Extended, CCBot), each allow or deny per
     tenant setting.
5. **Configuration, not code.** Thresholds, the enabled checks and the crawler policy are settings with market-pack
   defaults. A product may add checks through `@stackra/seo` extension points (for example, the commerce product's
   checks).
6. **UI split:**
   - `@stackra/ui` ships the parts (gap-inventory rows 61-64): search-result preview, counted field, social card preview,
     analysis checklist with score.
   - `@stackra/content` composes the "Search engine listing" section on every content type, per
     [0270](/adr/0270-builder-architecture-one-editor-kernel-document-kinds-and-collaboration-levels) decision 2.
   - The document editor shows the same analysis as a side-panel contribution.
7. **AI assist** (title, description, FAQ, alt text suggestions) comes from `@stackra/ai` actions. Suggestions are shown,
   never auto-applied.

## Consequences

- One analysis everywhere: product pages, posts, help articles and the storefront, in English and Arabic.
- Answer-engine readiness is measurable and shipped by default.
- Chores:
  - `@stackra/seo` gains a language-rules folder that needs native-speaker review for Arabic;
  - engine limits (title pixel width, description length) are settings that need updating when Google changes them.

## Enforcement

Planned:

- fixtures in `@stackra/seo` for every check in both languages;
- the content section's e2e test asserts the preview and counters update live.
