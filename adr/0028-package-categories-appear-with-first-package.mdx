## Context

`packages/` holds ten category folders. Nine of them contain only a `.gitkeep`; `experience` holds `@stackra/brand`. An
empty folder documents nothing a page cannot, and it suggests packages exist where none do. `pnpm new` already creates the
destination path recursively (`tools/scripts/scaffold.mjs`), so a category folder does not need to exist in advance.

## Decision

1. **The ten categories stay the taxonomy**: foundation, application, communication, cross-cutting, data, developer,
   experience, runtime, security, state. They are listed in `/guides/packages` and in `CATEGORIES` in `tools/scripts/scaffold.mjs`.
2. **Empty category folders are deleted**, with their `.gitkeep` files.
3. **A category folder is created with its first package**, by `pnpm new <kind> <name> --category <c>`.
4. **The archetypes of [0004](/adr/0004-package-archetypes-and-layers) are unchanged.**

## Consequences

- `ls packages` shows only what exists.
- The taxonomy lives in two places (the guide and `CATEGORIES`); they must agree.

## Enforcement

`tools/scripts/scaffold.mjs` rejects a `--category` outside `CATEGORIES`. Planned — a validator check that every directory
under `packages/` other than `tooling` contains at least one member, and that every category folder is in `CATEGORIES`.

## Supersedes and sources

Supersedes: none. Amends: none — [0001](/adr/0001-monorepo-layout) and [0004](/adr/0004-package-archetypes-and-layers) require
packages to sit in category folders but do not require empty ones. Applies 0004 decision 4 ("directories exist only when
something real lives in them") to `packages/`. Sources: owner decision in chat, 2026-10-19.
