## Context

Owner decisions R4-6, A3-1, RV-43 and US-43 split payments connections by how they reach the money: a merchant's own
Stripe account over OAuth with no platform fee, a merchant's own key at a provider without OAuth, a Figentra Pay account
the platform creates (with a fee schedule, a pre-verification cap and a payout hold), and accounts a product already runs
under its own Connect platform before cutover. `StripeConnection.onboard` refuses until the connection's
`connectAccountType` field is set, and the hand-over asked whether a platform-level default belongs in data. Stripe's own
guidance now favours describing a connected account by its controller properties rather than a legacy type, so a fixed
type in code would age badly.

## Decision

1. A payments Connection carries a `mode` field: `oauth-standard`, `own-key` (the default when absent),
   `connected-account` or `legacy-connected-account:<platform>`. `modeOf` reads it and refuses any other value.
2. The pre-verification cap and the payout hold apply to `connected-account` only. Their values come from the
   `ConnectedAccountLimits` port, which the host binds over the OS verification record and `fee-schedule:pay`;
   `PaymentService` asks it for no other mode.
3. `oauth-standard` and `connected-account` connections act on their `accountRef` with the platform's key through
   `Stripe-Account`. OAuth Standard onboarding and revocation are `StripeConnectIntegration.authorizeUrl`, `complete`
   and `revoke`; the merchant may also revoke from Stripe, which arrives as the platform's Connect webhook.
4. The account type used by `onboard` for `connected-account` mode is data: the market-pack key
   `payments.connect-account-type`, overridden by a Connection's `connectAccountType`. The seed value for packs that offer
   Figentra Pay on Stripe is `express`. A merchant-owned Standard account is never created by onboarding; it arrives
   through OAuth (`oauth-standard`). When neither the pack nor the Connection names a type, onboarding stays refused.
5. Wallets and pay-later on Stripe are the account's enabled payment methods, read from it (`paymentMethods`), never a
   driver or a list per Market.

## Consequences

The cap and hold cannot leak onto a merchant's own account, and a typo in a mode fails loudly instead of skipping the
cap. Packs gain one key; the `onboard` path must read the pack through the same source as `MarketPaymentDefaults`, which
is not wired yet. Products that cut over from their own Connect platform keep working under the legacy mode until they
migrate.

## Enforcement

`packages/commerce/payments/src/core/functions/__tests__/mode-of.function.test.ts` and the connection-mode cases in
`payment.service.test.ts` check decisions 1 and 2. planned — a pack-backed `connectAccountType` read in `onboard`.
