## Context

Grocery lanes, restaurant ordering kiosks and the Academorix entry kiosks run unattended. They must be usable by a
seated person and a blind person, reset when a visitor walks away, and lock the device to the app. `@stackra/platform`
had a `KioskMode` port and a desktop `kioskOnLaunch` default that nothing read, and `@stackra/ui` had no unattended
mode at all.

## Decision

1. **`KioskSurface` in `@stackra/ui`, web and native.** It wraps an unattended screen: it resets through the caller's
   `onIdle` after `idleTimeoutMs` with no touch, pointer or key; lays the screen out for a `KioskReach` (`standing`, or
   `seated` with every control in the lower band and larger targets); carries high contrast; and `useKiosk().announce`
   writes every announcement to a polite live region (web) or the screen reader (native) and, with audio on, speaks it
   (the browser's speech synthesis on web, the caller's `speak` on native). It decides nothing about sessions.
2. **The defaults are configuration, the values are device settings.** `KioskConfig` (`platform.kiosk`) holds
   `enterOnLaunch`, `idleTimeout` (90 seconds), `reach`, `isHighContrast` and `isAudioOn`; a device's settings, keyed by
   its device-catalogue kind and its venue, override them. No timeout or layout is a constant in a component.
3. **The kiosk lock on launch.** `enterKioskOnLaunch(kiosk, config, deviceSetting?)` enters `KioskMode` when the device
   setting (or the config) asks, answers a refusal instead of throwing so a till still starts, and leaves a lock that is
   already on. Each target's `createApp` (`@stackra/app`) calls it once after boot; on Electron it passes
   `{ enterOnLaunch: desktop.kioskOnLaunch }`.

## Consequences

- A product builds a kiosk screen from the ordinary components inside one `KioskSurface`; the same components work on a
  staffed till because `useKiosk()` answers null there.
- High contrast on web is the theme's `prefers-contrast: more` edge set on the surface; a full high-contrast palette
  needs a token mode (later, in `@stackra/tokens`).
- Native audio needs a speech driver; until one ships the caller passes `speak`.

## Enforcement

planned — the accessibility audit of the kiosk screens in the playground e2e suite (axe, light and dark, LTR and RTL);
the unit tests in `packages/design-system/ui/src/{react,native}/kiosk-surface/__tests__/` and
`packages/experience/platform/src/core/functions/__tests__/enter-kiosk-on-launch.function.test.ts`.
