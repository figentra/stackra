## Context

[0072](/adr/0072-soc2-groundwork) (Proposed) set SOC 2 groundwork without a timeline, a tool or a disclosure route. The owner's answers SC1 to SC4, SC11 and the compliance tooling (mem L550, L552, L554, 2026-10-20) fix them.

## Decision

1. **Frameworks (SC1).** SOC 2 Security and Availability. Type I at the first enterprise deal. Type II six months later.
2. **Reviews (SC2).** Access is reviewed quarterly.
3. **Testing (SC3).** A penetration test before general availability, and yearly after it. A nightly ZAP scan. A bug bounty after general availability.
4. **Tooling (compliance).** Comp AI, self-hosted or free until the managed package is needed at the first enterprise report, covering SOC 2, ISO 27001, GDPR and the UAE and KSA PDPL.
5. **Disclosure (SC11).** `SECURITY.md` in the repository, private reporting, a `security@` address, and a three-business-day acknowledgement.
6. **Carried forward.** The blocking security review and gitleaks.

## Consequences

- A customer asking for SOC 2 gets a dated plan, not a promise.
- The controls map is the single source for every framework, so each control is written once.

## Enforcement

planned — the controls map page (new) is kept current with the programme; the quarterly review is a Linear recurring issue.

## Alternatives

- SOC 2 plus ISO 27001 plus self-hosted Comp AI now: rejected by the gap analysis (E-10); the owner chose the staged plan.
