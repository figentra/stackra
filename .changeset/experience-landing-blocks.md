The thirteen blocks of the Stackra landing in `./react`, each with its `Block` class in `.`: `SiteHeaderBlock`,
`HeroMachineBlock`, `CtaRowBlock`, `StatementBlock`, `ChapterTitleBlock`, `StationBlock`, `StepSwitcherBlock`,
`BentoGridBlock`, `TerminalDemoBlock`, `MonoDisplayBlock`, `AskAiBlock`, `ProductCardsBlock` and `SiteFooterBlock`.
They draw the blueprint direction (ruled sections, schematic windows, the station rail) on `@stackra/ui` and its
blueprint token roles; every word comes in props, and each `Block` declares the document props as `LocalizedText`.
