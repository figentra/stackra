`stackra db diff` derives a model's trait columns from its traits, the static form of `traitsOf`. Each column-writing trait (`HasPublicId`, `Timestamps`, `UserStamps`, `SoftDeletes`, `Archivable`, `Versioned`, `HasTree`, `HasMetadata`, `BelongsToTenant`, `BelongsToTenantOrPlatform`, `HasOwner`) gives the columns and indexes its migration macro writes, with the macro's type and nullability, over the field the checker read: a `HasTree` model now agrees with `t.tree()` (`ltree` path, nullable on an existing table, GiST index, `varchar(33)` parent with its index) instead of reporting `column_type tree_path` and `undeclared_index idx_<table>_parent_id`, and a `Partitioned({ by })` model agrees with `t.partitionBy`'s BRIN index. The blanket tolerance of `tree_path` and `metadata` on every table is gone, so those columns on a table whose model lacks the trait are now `undeclared_column`, and a `HasTree` table whose path is not `ltree` or lacks its GiST index is reported. A `version` field is typed `integer` only under `Versioned`. New exports: `TRAIT_COLUMNS`, `traitColumnsOf` and the `TraitColumns` type; `SchemaSnapshot` columns may say `nullable: "either"` and indexes `optional`. Discovery keeps the constant properties of a trait's object argument when another is not constant (`Partitioned({ by, retain: Duration.days(400) })` keeps `by`).
