Consent proofs can now be kept durably, and a guardian's consent can be verified by signing in (STA-2818, ADR 0454 note of 2026-11-26). `@stackra/consent/node` adds `PostgresConsentProofStoreDriver` (`postgres`) with the `ConsentProofEntry` model, its migration (`consent.consent_proof_entries`) and `CONSENT_ENTITIES`: tenant-owned under the forced `tenant_id` policy, append-only (UPDATE revoked and refused by a trigger), each subject's proofs a hash chain appended in a savepoint of the caller's transaction, with `ConsentProofWriteFailedError` after five lost races. `linkConsentProof` and `findConsentProofChainBreak` build and check that chain. The new `guardian_login` verifier, `GuardianLoginConsentVerifierDriver`, passes only a token `GuardianLoginAttestationIssuer` signed from the call's verified assurance within `guardianLoginMaxAgeMinutes` (default 5) of the sign-in, bound to the guardian, the person and the document and valid for `guardianLoginAttestationMinutes` (default 10); the proof keeps its id as the verification reference. `./testing` adds `describeConsentProofStoreConformance`. The default verifier stays `none` and the default store stays `memory`.
