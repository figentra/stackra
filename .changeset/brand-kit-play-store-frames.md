`DEVICE_FRAMES` now draws Play-accepted Android frames: `android_phone` is 1080x1920 (1080x2400 is over Play's 2:1
ratio) and two tablet frames are added, `android_tablet_7` (1200x1920) and `android_tablet_10` (1600x2560). The iOS
frames move to 1290x2796 and 2048x2732, the sizes the release store check generates (STA-1577).
