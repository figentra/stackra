The rules come from [0353](/adr/0353-data-residency-a-region-fixed-at-sign-up-a-login-directory-that-only-routes-and-copies-within-a-jurisdiction).
Your deployment chooses its regions; Stackra keeps each tenant inside the one it picked.

## Regions

- A deployment declares its regions as short keys in its runway configuration, each with its cloud (AWS or GCP) and
  cloud region ([0354](/adr/0354-two-clouds-aws-and-gcp-and-one-aws-account-per-product-region)). Each region of a
  product gets its own cloud account.
- A tenant picks its region at sign-up, and it never changes. Moving region is an export and an import into a new
  tenant.
- Each region runs its own stack with its own database, storage and identity provider instance. Regions are never
  active-active.

## What never crosses a region

- **Tenant data.** No tenant row or file is replicated or copied to another region. The single exception is an item a
  user explicitly shares with another tenant, which is audited and checked against the sending entity's transfer
  rules.
- **Backups.** Point-in-time recovery, snapshots and object replication stay in the tenant's jurisdiction: a second
  account, zone or region of the same jurisdiction, never another
  ([0186](/adr/0186-backups-and-disaster-recovery), as amended). `@stackra/infra-aws` refuses an environment whose
  backups or replicated files would leave the foundation's region
  ([0402](/adr/0402-infra-backups-in-jurisdiction-static-egress-status-page-runtime-stack-health-and-storefront-edge)
  decision 1).
- **Sign-in.** Authentication always runs in the tenant's region.
- **Misrouted calls.** `RegionGuardDriver` (`@stackra/tenancy`) is the first step of every request and job: a call for
  a tenant placed in another region than the deployment's fails loudly instead of reading or writing out of region.
  It fails closed when a tenant has no placement.

Cross-region operator views, such as audit, query each region and copy nothing centrally.

## Backups and recovery

| Item                             | Value (designed defaults, [0186](/adr/0186-backups-and-disaster-recovery)) |
| -------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Point-in-time recovery           | 14 days                                                                    |
| Snapshots                        | 35 days, in a separate backup account, inside the jurisdiction             |
| Recovery point / time objectives | 15 minutes / 4 hours, per region                                           |
| Restore drill                    | quarterly                                                                  |
| One tenant's restore             | from an isolated point-in-time copy, then copied back                      |

These are design targets your deployment configures and drills; they are not a service level. Whole-region loss is
the deployment's choice, stated in its SLA.

## Logs and telemetry

Logs, traces and metrics leave every service as OpenTelemetry (OTLP) to the destination your deployment configures.
They carry no personal data; the designed retention is 30 days hot and 13 months in object storage
([0184](/adr/0184-telemetry-opentelemetry-otlp-slis-heartbeats-and-log-retention)). Put the destination and its
storage in the same jurisdiction as the region if your contracts require it; Stackra does not pin it.

## Vendors without a region

A vendor that carries no tenant records of substance (email delivery, crash reports, product analytics without
personal data) may serve every region from one jurisdiction and must appear on the sub-processor list. AI processing
is routed per tenant region under the rule the product sets. Every new vendor is checked against these rules.

## The login directory

Before sign-in, one directory routes a person to their region. It holds only keyed hashes (HMAC-SHA-256 of the
normalised email or phone) mapped to region keys, never a readable identifier, so it is replicated everywhere and
survives the loss of any region ([0451](/adr/0451-the-login-directory-an-identity-format-and-writer-and-a-cloudflare-worker-of-its-own)).

## The package registry

The Stackra registry holds no tenant data. For a Pro licence it keeps an opaque reference to the customer account,
token hashes and daily download counts per licence; it stores no IP address. It runs on Cloudflare and is not tied to
any of your regions.

## Not finished

<Warning>
  `TenantPlacement` (where each tenant lives) and `LoginDirectoryStore` (the directory writer's
  store) ship with in-memory drivers only. They are fine for tests and a single process; a
  production deployment with more than one instance needs persistent drivers, which are tracked in
  STA-1473. Until they ship, the region guard is only as reliable as the placement driver your host
  binds.
</Warning>

- Where the region keys are declared (runway or tenancy configuration) and the lint that checks a `region` value
  against them are open (0353 open question 1).
- Figentra's own regions and per-plan choices are Figentra OS decisions, not Stackra's.
