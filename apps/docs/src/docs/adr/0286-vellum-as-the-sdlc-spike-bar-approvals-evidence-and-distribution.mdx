## Context

Vellum (figentra/vellum, a separate product) enforces spec lifecycles: approvals are signed, and task evidence is
recorded in a hash-chained ledger in git. [0282](/adr/0282-developer-experience-skills-cli-mcp-and-vellum-as-the-sdlc)
makes Vellum the SDLC only if spike STA-298 passes, and until then spec-kit stays. The owner's decisions of
2026-10-22 answer the questions 0282 left open: how Vellum is distributed, who signs approvals, where task commands
run, who owns the renderers, the licence and visibility, and the bar the spike must clear. Each decision below
records its reasoning, so the page can be read without other documents.

## Decision

### Spike and adoption

1. **Save the uncommitted Vellum work first (VL1).** Each uncommitted working tree is committed as it is to a
   `wip/<branch>` branch, pushed to figentra/vellum, and then re-cloned. A local copy is deleted only after its pushes
   are verified. Nothing unpushed is deleted. Broken worktree links make an accidental prune likely, and committing
   as-is keeps each change reviewable.
2. **The pass bar for STA-298 (VL3).** STA-298 passes only if its written criteria hold and four further checks hold:
   - an agent session cannot produce a valid approval, because no signing key is reachable from it. Decided reading: this binds builder sessions, which cannot reach the reviewer key, and the reviewer identity of decision 4 is the only identity that signs;
   - evidence recorded on the box or the remote runner verifies on the owner's machine;
   - the Quality Gate adds 60 seconds or less;
   - one full issue costs the lead no more than 15 extra minutes compared with spec-kit.

   The bar is set before the spike starts, so the spike cannot turn into adoption by default.

3. **Adoption follows a pass.** If STA-298 passes, a follow-up ADR supersedes
   [0067](/adr/0067-spec-kit-is-the-sdlc) and decision D15 of
   [0121](/adr/0121-sdlc-tooling-roles-and-linear), as decision 11 of 0282 says. If it fails, decision 14 of 0282
   applies: spec-kit stays and Vellum is only the approval and evidence layer.

### Approvals and lanes

4. **Approvals are given by the reviewer identity (VL4).** The separate reviewer identity approves Vellum tasks by
   default. Its signing key sits in an Infisical path that builder agents cannot read (STA-238). The owner approves only changes at change-risk R3, the top class of [0120](/adr/0120-sdlc-spec-location-lanes-and-change-risk), which includes auth, iam, tenancy, money, PII, secrets, webhooks, crypto, CI permissions and agent guards. Releases stay owner-approved under [0119](/adr/0119-merge-policy-and-release-authority) decision 5, which this ADR does not change. There is no hardware-key ceremony. This is not one
   of the three options that were weighed: the reviewer key is extended from low-risk lanes to every lane below R3.
   The reason is the owner's stated aim of minimal human interaction, with the owner's time kept for R3 and above.
   Builders never approve their own work ([0230](/adr/0230-reviewer-identity-separate-from-builder)).
5. **Lanes need different proof (VL5).** The full and lite lanes need a signed approval and recorded evidence. The
   bug lane records evidence only, because its failing test is already the evidence. The trivial lane needs neither.
   The lanes are those of [0120](/adr/0120-sdlc-spec-location-lanes-and-change-risk).

### Packages and distribution

6. **The renderers belong to Stackra (VL6).** The renderer layer moves into this repository as the published
   `@stackra/renderers` package in the developer category, and Vellum depends on it from npmjs. `@vellum/renderers`
   is no longer the shared package. The reason: Stackra is public, has release checks and a fixed version
   ([0177](/adr/0177-package-distribution-npmjs-and-keygen)), and is the larger consumer, while a public package should
   not depend on a product whose distribution is still open. The developer packages become four: `@stackra/skills`,
   `@stackra/cli`, `@stackra/mcp` and `@stackra/renderers`.
7. **One public package (VL8).** Vellum is distributed as one public package, `@figentra/vellum`, published to npmjs
   with provenance. It bundles the internal `@vellum/*` packages, which stay workspace-private. Vellum leaves the
   private registry.
8. **Private until 1.0 (VL9).** figentra/vellum stays private until version 1.0, then goes public. Its MIT licence text
   is already in place, so the later change costs nothing. The legal name on its licence follows
   [0291](/adr/0291-porting-provenance-and-the-third-party-reading-rule).
9. **Free for now (VL10).** Vellum is free and MIT-licensed as an adoption path into Stackra. Whether team features
   become paid is reviewed when the first team asks. No price is set.
10. **Brand direction approved, nothing published (VL16).** The Vellum brand direction is approved. Nothing is
    published until the repository is public (decision 8). The brand kit follows
    [0291](/adr/0291-porting-provenance-and-the-third-party-reading-rule).

### Task commands, merge and specs

11. **Task commands run on the remote runner (VL7).** `vellum task complete --command` runs through the remote runner
    of [0287](/adr/0287-agent-sandboxes-and-the-remote-runner) (STA-255). Vellum records the runner job id, the git tree
    hash, the exit status and an output digest. Light commands may still run locally. Remote evidence is a Vellum
    feature, filed as an STA issue (decision 14).
12. **`pr:merge` stays the only merger (VL13).** `vellum verify` is a step inside the required Quality Gate, and
    Vellum's `automatedMergePermitted` stays off in Stackra's policy. [0119](/adr/0119-merge-policy-and-release-authority)
    and [0166](/adr/0166-ci-policy-runners-budgets-concurrency-and-check-matrix) are unchanged.
13. **Existing specs (VL14).** If the spike passes, `vellum adopt` runs on open specs only. Done specs stay frozen as
    spec-kit history, with no retroactive approvals, because retroactive approvals would be fake evidence.

### Toolchain, assistants and tracking

14. **Tracking (VL/OP5, amends 0282 decision 16).** Vellum work is tracked in Linear team STA with the `vellum` label.
    The code lives in figentra/vellum, and branches follow Linear's naming, `figentra/sta-<n>-<slug>`, in that
    repository, so `pnpm linear` works unchanged. Gaps such as `vellum sync` are STA issues, not issues in
    figentra/vellum.
15. **Preset split (VL2, amends 0282 decision 12).** Vellum's engine stays in figentra/vellum. The open Stackra preset,
    which holds the Impact questionnaire and the risk and lane rules, ships in `@stackra/skills`. Linear and the
    internal process rules stay in `.agents/` in this repository. The preset is not a `presets/stackra` folder in
    figentra/vellum.
16. **Assistants (VL12).** Claude Code and OpenCode are supported and tested in the spike. The Kiro and Cursor renderers
    are kept and marked untested.
17. **Toolchain (VL11).** After the spike passes, Vellum moves to Node 24, pnpm 12, oxfmt (Prettier is dropped), tsdown,
    the published `@stackra/*-config` presets and the runway reusable workflows. The formatter-proof task parser must
    pass under oxfmt first. Until the spike has passed, Vellum keeps its own toolchain.
18. **Repositories (VL15).** Stackra is the first repository, for the spike, and Figentra OS follows once the spike
    passes. Academorix and the other product repositories stay out until someone asks.

## Consequences

- Vellum gains a remote-evidence feature, a reviewer-identity approval path and the `vellum` label in Linear.
- Approvals depend on the reviewer key path (STA-238). Until that path exists, decision 4 cannot be met, and no task can
  be approved under it.
- The renderer layer has a second consumer in `@stackra/renderers`, so a change to it must keep Stackra and Vellum
  working.
- If STA-298 fails, decisions 3 (adoption), 13 (adopt specs) and 17 (toolchain) do not apply, and spec-kit stays.

## Enforcement

planned — `vellum verify` inside the Quality Gate once STA-298 passes (decision 12). The four checks of decision 2 are
the spike's own tests, run on the box and the Mac. The lane rules of decision 5 move into `tools/scripts/spec-gate.mjs`
when Vellum replaces spec-kit. No check yet enforces decision 4.

## Dependencies

The reviewer key path (STA-238) is not built. Today both bot apps' credentials sit in the same Infisical folder on one machine ([0230](/adr/0230-reviewer-identity-separate-from-builder)). Decision 4 needs a path that builder agents cannot read, which is stricter than today, so decision 4 cannot be met until STA-238 delivers that path.
