## Context

0366 supported a subset of RRULE and refused the rest, read UNTIL by its date only, counted skipped dates toward COUNT,
and left callers to pass `BusinessCalendar` data until a pack loader existed. Products need monthly "last working day"
runs (BYSETPOS), a time of day per instance (BYHOUR), week-numbered schedules and an exact end instant. Owner question 5
asked for "ten sessions" to stay ten when a holiday falls on one. The US pack needs federal and state holidays, which
are weekday positions (third Monday of January), observed on a weekday when they fall on a weekend, and need
`uncertainDays` and the 29 February rule as pack values.

## Decision

1. **RRULE.** Supported: FREQ DAILY to YEARLY, INTERVAL, COUNT, UNTIL (a date, or a date and time in UTC or in the
   series' wall time), BYDAY (positions in the month, or in the year for YEARLY without BYMONTH), BYMONTHDAY, BYMONTH,
   BYYEARDAY, BYWEEKNO, BYHOUR, BYMINUTE, BYSECOND, BYSETPOS and WKST. A rule is expanded period by period and
   BYSETPOS picks within a period; instances before the series start are neither returned nor counted.
2. **Refused on purpose, with the reason in the error.** FREQ HOURLY, MINUTELY and SECONDLY (a series is dates at wall
   times; repetition within a day is working hours or slots); RSCALE and SKIP of RFC 7529 (a yearly Hijri date is a
   holiday rule of kind `hijri`, converted by ICU); BYSECOND=60 (no instant in ECMAScript time). Also refused, as
   RFC 5545 forbids: an unknown or repeated part, COUNT with UNTIL, BYYEARDAY with DAILY, WEEKLY or MONTHLY, BYWEEKNO
   outside YEARLY, BYMONTHDAY with WEEKLY, a BYDAY position with DAILY, WEEKLY or BYWEEKNO, and BYSETPOS alone.
3. **COUNT mode.** `countMode` is `generated` (RFC 5545, the default: excepted and holiday dates count) or `kept`
   (only kept dates count). Which a product uses is its setting.
4. **Holiday rules gain a weekday position and an observed shift.** Kind `nth_weekday`: the n-th or n-th last weekday
   of a month plus an offset in days. A yearly `gregorian` or `nth_weekday` rule may carry `observe`: per weekday, a
   shift in days, possibly into the year before.
5. **Calendar data from packs.** `calendarFromPack(resolved, on, local)` builds the `BusinessCalendar` from the pack
   keys `calendar.time_zone`, `calendar.working_week`, `calendar.uncertain_days` and every `calendar.holidays.<set>`
   (joined across overlays), with the location's zone, week and closures laid over it. `leapBirthdayFromPack` reads
   `calendar.leap_birthday`. A missing key is refused, never defaulted. The calendar's subject is `location` in the
   seed packs (0385).
6. **First pack values, as draft.** The `us` seed pack sets `calendar.uncertain_days` to `working` (no US holiday is
   sighted) and `calendar.leap_birthday` to `march_1`, the federal holiday set, and a Monday-to-Friday 09:00-17:00
   default week; the seven state overlays set their state holiday sets. All are `draft` and need counsel's sign-off.
7. **Hermes ICU is a note, not code.** Whether the till's Hermes build ships the `islamic-umalqura` calendar and time
   zones is checked on a release build (README "Limits"); the package still throws rather than fall back.

## Consequences

- Recurring payroll, billing and class schedules can be written as standard RRULE text; a refused part says why.
- A US calendar is pack data; a state that adds or moves a holiday is a pack edit.
- `@stackra/calendar` depends on `@stackra/market-packs`.
- Values for markets with sighted holidays (`sa`, `ae`, `eg`) still need an owner answer to wave 10 question 2.
