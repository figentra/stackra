## Context

`stackra seed-pack` plans a pack against what a member already stores and needs `seedState` and `applySeed` from
`@stackra/database/node`; without them a run failed unless it was `--check`. The action pipeline's idempotency and
rate-limit ports had memory and Valkey drivers only, and their own docblocks put the durable Postgres driver in the
database package, since `@stackra/actions` must never import it.

## Decision

1. **A seed ledger.** `database.seed_records` holds one row per record of a product seed pack: kind, key, profile,
   pack version, content hash, owner (`seed` or `tenant`) and the rows. `seedState` reads a pack's rows; `applySeed`
   writes the planned records as product defaults in one transaction and never overwrites a row whose owner is
   `tenant`, even from a stale plan. The owning package reads its product defaults from the ledger by kind and key and
   flips the owner when someone edits a default. The connection is the member's `DATABASE_URL`, as for `db migrate`.
2. **Postgres action stores.** `PostgresIdempotencyStoreDriver` and `PostgresRateLimitStoreDriver` (driver name
   `postgres`) keep `database.idempotency_keys` and `database.rate_limit_buckets`. Every call commits in its own
   transaction. A claim is one upsert that takes a free or expired key and leaves a live one untouched; a take locks
   its bucket row. Each bounds its table by sweeping at most 100 expired rows per call. The tables carry no row-level
   security: a key is already scoped by action, tenant and actor, and the pipeline claims before the scope session
   opens.

## Consequences

- `stackra seed-pack` runs end to end once `stackra db migrate` has applied this package's migrations.
- A deployment without Valkey keeps idempotency and rate limits across instances on its own Postgres.
- `@stackra/actions` must list `postgres` among its store driver names before runway can select it.
- Writing each seeded record into its owning package's own store (settings layers, rule sets) is that package's
  reader of the ledger, not this runner's.
