## Context

The first markets require fiscal documents by law: clearance and reporting e-invoicing with a device certificate per
till (ZATCA Phase 2), sealed documents with registered item codes (the Egyptian e-invoicing system), tax invoices
through an accredited service provider or the Peppol network, and hybrid PDF invoices (Factur-X, ZUGFeRD). Commerce's
"EU on day one" (COM-36) adds the till fiscal rules: a technical security element, a certified software chain, a
fiscal data module, a telematic recorder, a register security regulation and a verifiable billing record. The owner
approved `@stackra/fiscal` for e-invoicing (questionnaire #21); the configuration principle
([0363](/adr/0363-configuration-over-code-business-behaviour-is-data-rules-workflows-and-studios)) says no package names
a country, so which of these applies is market-pack data. Nothing was on disk.

## Decision

1. **One package, one port, two channels.** `@stackra/fiscal` (`packages/commerce/fiscal`, `tier:lib`) holds every
   e-invoicing mandate and every till fiscal device behind one `FiscalDriver` port (`seal`, `submit`,
   `exportJournal`). A document is sealed in the `invoice` channel (the pack's e-invoicing mandate) or the `till`
   channel (the pack's till fiscal rule). The first drivers are `zatca`, `eta`, `peppol`, `factur-x`, `tse`, `nf525`,
   `fdm`, `rt`, `rksv` and `verifactu`.
2. **The pack chooses.** A market pack's fiscal section names, per channel, the driver key, its options, the offline
   reporting window and the retention period. Each driver validates its options with its own schema and refuses a
   pack that leaves one out; nothing has a default in code. A market served through a provider (an ASP, an access
   point) is the same driver with that provider's endpoint in the pack. The pack is read through the
   `FiscalPackSource` port until the market-pack loader has a home.
3. **Seal offline, submit later.** `FiscalService.seal` signs and chains a document onto its unit's journal with no
   network, idempotently per document; `submitPending` hands sealed records to the authority in sequence order and
   stops at the first it cannot reach. `offlineStatus` reports the backlog against the pack's reporting window, so a
   till stops issuing at the lower of the tenant's offline limit and that window.
4. **Keys, credentials and the network stay with the host.** Signing (`FiscalSigner`: XAdES, CAdES, JWS, raw), the
   turnover cipher, HTTP with its credentials (`FiscalTransport`) and the per-unit credential store (`TillCredentials`)
   are ports. A CSID per till is onboarded by `ZatcaOnboarding` (compliance CSID, compliance check, production CSID),
   whose issued secrets go straight to the store.
5. **Hardware through a port, pairing in devices.** TSEs, fiscal data modules and telematic recorders are reached
   through `FiscalDevice`; their pairing and registry are `@stackra/devices` (handover conflict 3).
6. **Hybrid PDF through documents.** The CII XML is built here; embedding it in a PDF/A-3 is the `HybridPdf` port,
   bound by `@stackra/documents`.
7. **Journal export per mandate.** Each driver exports its unit's records in its mandate's audit form (a device
   archive, a signed-receipt file, or JSON lines of the chain).
8. **Format identity is code, market values are data.** Standard namespaces, UNCL code lists and a format's own type
   letters are in the drivers; endpoints, versions, profile ids, tax and payment mappings, slots, buckets and time
   zones are pack options.
9. **No tax computation here.** Lines arrive with tax computed (OS invoicing and pricing from pack rates) and numbers
   from the host's sequences; fiscal never recomputes or numbers.

## Consequences

- A new market is a pack (and, for a new mandate, one driver), never a branch in code.
- Hosts write five bindings (pack source, journal store, signer, transport, credential store) plus device and PDF
  bindings where a driver needs them; fakes for each ship in `./testing`.
- Each driver's output is checked against the authority's sandbox in the certification tracker (COM-356) before its
  pack goes live.

## Enforcement

planned — the package's unit tests pin each driver's format, chain and refusals; the 0363 lint rule flags country
codes in package source; certification against each authority's sandbox is a pack launch-gate item.

## Open questions

1. **ETA e-receipts.** The B2C receipt flow (receipt UUID chain and its QR) is not a driver yet; whether it is a second
   ETA driver or an `eta` option is not decided.
2. **Validation polling.** Authorities that validate after accepting (the ETA document status) need a status query;
   its home (driver method or workflow) is not decided.
3. **Pack home.** The `FiscalPackSource` binding waits for the market-pack loader (0363 open question 1).
