## Context

The owner ruled on 2026-10-25 that countries are market packs: versioned data holding tax, e-invoicing, till fiscal
rules, legal floors, providers, sign-in methods, calendar, formats and price tier. OS pricing, invoicing, identity,
verification, notifications, geography and tenancy, and every product, need to read that data the same way: verified,
overlaid (`eu` then `eu-de`, `ae` then `ae-difc`), dated (a VAT change on 1 July), pinned per tenant, and with the
floors no setting may cross. Juristra also needs reviewer sign-off per rule and a launch gate per pack and product.
Nothing is on disk, and 0363 left the home open. Alternatives weighed: a part of `@stackra/settings` (rejected: settings
is per-scope state, while packs are signed per-market data that the rules engine and offline tills read too) and a
pack format inside `@stackra/assets` (rejected: assets resolves files; it does not know versions, overlays or dates).

## Decision

1. **Package.** `@stackra/market-packs` in `packages/foundation/market-packs`, `tier:lib`, entry `.` only, depending on
   `@stackra/contracts`, `@stackra/errors` and `zod`. Pure mechanism, isomorphic and offline; it ships no pack content.
   Base packs are owned by the platform (OS), product sections by each product.
2. **Signed and content-hashed.** A pack ships as `{ pack, hash, signature }`: the SHA-256 of the canonical JSON of the
   pack (keys sorted at every depth) and an Ed25519 signature of that hash by a named key. `signPack` is the build
   step; `verifyPack` checks shape, hash (over the pack exactly as shipped) and signature against trusted public keys
   from deployment configuration, and refuses with `PackRejectedError` (`shape_invalid`, `hash_mismatch`,
   `key_unknown`, `signature_invalid`). A service whose packs do not verify does not start. Crypto is Web Crypto.
3. **Overlays.** A pack names the market it overlays in `extends`. `resolvePack` walks the chain to the root and
   returns the layers root first. A layer's value wins over its parent's only while its own entry is in force. A loop
   is refused (`overlay_cycle`); a market with no fitting version is `PackNotFoundError`.
4. **Versions, status and pins.** A pack version is `major.minor.patch` with a status: `draft`, `beta`, `live`,
   `retired`. Which markets are open is this status, never a list in code. A tenant pins per market to a major line
   (`"2"`) or, for a rollback, one exact version (`"2.1.0"`); an unpinned market takes its highest accepted version.
   Only `live` is accepted unless the caller says otherwise (a sandbox may accept `beta`, per 0359). A major release
   changes shape and is promoted like any structural change.
5. **Dated values.** An entry is a key, a JSON value and the days it is in force (`from`, optional `until`, both
   inclusive); the latest start in force wins. `valueOf(resolved, key, on)` answers "the value of X for market M on
   date D" with the market and version that set it; `valuesOn` gives all of them. A consumer checks a value against
   its own schema (for example `BusinessCalendar$` of 0366).
6. **Legal floors as data.** A floor entry carries `bound` (`min` or `max`) and `law`. `floorsOn` lists the floors in
   force with their pack, so the settings resolver (0213 cascade plus a floor) and the rules engine (0331, as a final
   guard) refuse a value past the floor and say which pack and law set it. `factsOn` gives the values in force as a
   plain record, a fact source for rules. This package carries floors; it does not apply them.
7. **Product sections.** A pack with `section` set is one product's part of a market (courts, safeguarding,
   hallmarking). Its keys start with `<section>.`, so it never overwrites a base value; it has no `extends` of its own
   and follows its base pack's major line. A tenant resolves the sections its products need.
8. **Review and launch gate.** A pack version records reviewer sign-offs per entry key and a launch checklist whose
   items may name a product. Both are signed with the version, so completing an item is a new patch.
   `unreviewed` lists keys without a sign-off; `launchGate` says whether a product may sell under a resolved market:
   every layer `live`, every checklist item for that product done, every key reviewed.
9. **Not here.** Applying a floor to a setting stays with `@stackra/settings`; rule evaluation with `@stackra/rules`;
   provider choice per Connection with `@stackra/integrations` (0356); where pack bytes are stored and fetched with the
   caller; which pack wins for a tenant active in two markets stays 0363 open question 3.

## Consequences

- A new market is a new pack (and drivers where needed); a dated legal change is a new minor version published ahead.
- Every refusal over a floor can name the pack, version and law.
- A tampered or unsigned pack stops a service at start-up rather than changing tax or legal behaviour silently.
- The calendar (0366), settings and rules can drop their "until the pack loader exists" caveats once they read through
  this package.
- Checklist and review changes need a re-signed version; counsel signs pack versions, not ADRs.

## Enforcement

planned — the package's tests (`packages/foundation/market-packs/src/**/__tests__/`) fix the behaviour with made-up
markets only; the lint rule of
[0363](/adr/0363-configuration-over-code-business-behaviour-is-data-rules-workflows-and-studios) will flag market
codes and values in source. Nothing checks it in CI yet.
