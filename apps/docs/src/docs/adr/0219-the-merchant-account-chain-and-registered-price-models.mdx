description: "Accepted · 2026-10-20"

## Context

A customer sells through a chain of accounts: Figentra, then the product operator, then the tenant, then the end customer. Fees and payments move through that chain. The owner's merchant answers (mem L520–529, 2026-10-20) fix the payment modes, the fee cascade, the ledger rule and the pricing builder.

## Decision

1. **Chain.** The merchant chain runs Figentra, then product operator, then tenant, then end customer. It is the tenant tree of ADR 0210.
2. **Payment modes per merchant.** Platform Connect with fees, bring-your-own keys, direct merchant, and B2B invoicing.
3. **Fees.** Fee rules cascade down the chain. Every movement goes through the ledger, per party (ADR 0220).
4. **Pricing builder.** The no-code builder is made of registered blocks. Developer-registered `@PriceModel` code covers the rest. Tenant data holds no expressions (mem L529; contradiction C-23).
5. **Price resolution (PR, mem L792).** The precedence of price resolution belongs to the OS pricing service.

## Consequences

- A tenant cannot write a price expression, so a price is always code the platform has reviewed.
- Every fee is traceable to the chain level that set it.

## Enforcement

planned — the fee cascade test covers each payment mode; the validator rejects an expression in tenant pricing data.

## Supersedes and sources

Supersedes: none. Sources: owner merchant answers (`stackra-decisions-2026-10-19.md` L520–529, L792, 2026-10-20). Contradiction C-23.
