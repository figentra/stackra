## Context

Commerce, Muslin, Academorix fees, Juristra invoices and Figentra's own billing all take payments. Academorix (Stripe
Connect, dunning, disputes) and Muslin (split tender, gift cards, Paymob, Tabby) each carry their own payment code, and
no gateway port exists in Stackra. [0219](/adr/0219-the-merchant-account-chain-and-registered-price-models) fixes the
merchant chain, the payment modes and the fee cascade, and [0220](/adr/0220-stackra-money-and-the-ledger-package) the
money and ledger primitives, but not the shape of the payment ports. The owner decided the architecture on 2026-10-25
(Commerce questionnaire Answers and 27 to 30; OS rounds 5, 13 and 14) and, in round 22, that Stackra keeps only the
mechanism ([0259](/adr/0259-module-placement-three-homes)). Figentra Pay, the providers per market, fees and
Figentra's legal role are Figentra's choices, recorded in Figentra OS
[ADR 0034](https://docs.os.figentra.com/adr/0034-payments-architecture-one-engine-three-layers-and-providers) and
[ADR 0035](https://docs.os.figentra.com/adr/0035-figentra-pay-fees-wallets-refunds-disputes-and-reconciliation).

## Decision

**Ports and one engine.**

1. `@stackra/payments` holds the payment ports. A product never carries its own payment code: it calls the payments
   engine built on these ports (for Figentra, the OS payments and payments-ledger services). Academorix and Muslin code
   is not mixed in.
2. The ports serve three layers:
   - **gateway drivers**: one driver per provider behind the gateway port, chosen per tenant and Market through the
     Connection ([0356](/adr/0356-choosing-a-provider-per-tenant-and-market-through-the-connection-with-a-market-default));
   - **a platform-payments layer**: a Connect-style port for merchant onboarding with KYC, splits, the platform fee and
     payouts, served by a licensed provider's platform product;
   - **a branded payments surface**: checkout, saved methods, payment links, refunds and wallets, which a product shows
     in its own brand. One engine serves every brand.
3. **Merchant accounts.** The port serves both a merchant's own gateway account and the platform's account
   ([0219](/adr/0219-the-merchant-account-chain-and-registered-price-models) decision 2). A platform fee posts through
   the ledger and is reversed pro rata on a refund when the product charges one.

**Closed-loop wallet.**

4. **Ledger kinds.** Stored value is closed-loop only: store credit, gift cards, loyalty and a prepaid balance at the
   same merchant, each a ledger account kind in payments-ledger ([0220](/adr/0220-stackra-money-and-the-ledger-package)
   decision 4). The wallet actions offer no transfer between people and no cash-out; top-up is only buying a gift card
   or a prepaid balance, under a cap per Market. Loyalty is a shared kit (OS-343). An open wallet needs a new ADR.

**Safety rules.**

5. **PCI SAQ-A** (Commerce 28). Hosted fields or redirect only; the platform never touches card numbers. Terminals are
   semi-integrated. A saved card is a gateway token.
6. **The `unknown` state** (Commerce 29). A payment whose result is lost (for example a terminal that drops) enters an
   `unknown` state. It is resolved by a lookup at the provider before any retry. A manager may resolve it by hand,
   with a reason.
7. **KYC reuse as a port** (OS-54). Business verification sits behind a port: a business is checked once, every
   product and every payments brand reuses the result, and it is passed to the payment provider's own KYC. The design
   holds that only the licensed provider holds money, never the platform.
8. Sandbox gateways: see [0359](/adr/0359-modes-and-configuration-environments-display-modes-sandbox-promotion-and-live-records) decision 11.

**Cash.**

9. **Cash rounding** (Commerce answer 34). A `@stackra/money` table per currency and market sets the rounding step. It
   applies to cash only, and the difference is posted on its own ledger line.

## Consequences

- One payment engine to build, certify and secure, instead of one per product.
- Every product gets platform payments, wallets and payouts under its own brand without writing payment code.
- The gateway port must serve a merchant's own provider and the platform provider in the same Market.
- Academorix and Muslin move onto the engine at their rewrite.

## Enforcement

planned — the gateway, platform-layer, dispute and settlement ports in `@stackra/payments` with a conformance suite
per driver (including the `unknown` state and lookup-before-retry); a log and access-log redaction test that no card
number is ever accepted or logged; the validator check of round-3 #38 that no vendor SDK is reached outside a driver;
the wallet actions refusing transfer and cash-out.
