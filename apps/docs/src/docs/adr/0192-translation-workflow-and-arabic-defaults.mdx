<Note>
  Amended 2026-10-28 (owner decision, STA-1371): the digits of decision 2 are a per-customer setting.
  `I18nConfig.numerals` is the default (`latn`, Latin 0-9); a customer, tenant or user switches to
  `arab` (Arabic-Indic ٠-٩) through `I18nProvider`'s `numerals`, taken where `scopeChoosesNumerals`
  allows. Every shared formatter writes the setting on every engine (Hermes on iOS ignores the
  `-u-nu-` keyword): `formatMessage` numbers, plurals and dates, `formatNumber`, `formatDateTime`,
  `useNumber`, `useMoney`, `useDateTime`, the date labels and the charts of `@stackra/ui`. No screen
  forces Arabic-Indic digits because its text is Arabic. Decisions 1, 3 and 4 stand.
</Note>

<Note>
  Amended 2026-10-24 (STA-456): the calendars of decision 3 are named. The calendar systems are
  `gregory` (default) and `islamic-umalqura`, chosen per user (decision 3 makes Hijri optional; this
  names the two systems), and a dual display shows both. `@stackra/i18n` formats dates in the chosen
  calendar, and the date fields and pickers of `@stackra/ui` take the calendar from the locale
  settings. Decisions 1, 2 and 4 stand.
</Note>

## Context

[0080](/adr/0080-i18n-is-the-figentra-i18n-port) (Proposed) ported i18n and left the workflow open. The owner's answers PP10, PP11 and LO1 (mem L825, L562, 2026-10-20) fix the translation workflow, the Arabic defaults and where UI strings live.

## Decision

1. **Workflow (PP10).** English catalogues are the source. A steward pre-translates into other languages. A native Arabic reviewer approves Arabic. Tolgee is added at the third locale. The connectors are ADR 0236.
2. **Digits (PP11).** Latin digits are the default. Arabic-Indic digits are optional.
3. **Calendar (PP11).** Gregorian is the default. Hijri is optional.
4. **Storage (LO1).** UI strings stay in the i18n catalogues, not in a separate store.

## Consequences

- An Arabic string is reviewed by a native speaker before it ships, so pre-translation is a draft.
- Digits and calendar are user settings, so the defaults do not force a choice on anyone.

## Enforcement

planned — the catalogue check fails a locale with missing keys in CI; the Arabic review status is a field in the catalogue.

## Alternatives

- Machine translation without review: rejected by PP10.
