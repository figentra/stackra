## Context

`@stackra/segments` declares one action, `segments.segment.recompute@1` (`RecomputeSegmentsAction`), the hourly
membership recompute. Its only actor is `ActorKind.Job`. gen refuses it with `permission_unknown`, because no
`Permissions` class declares `segments.segment.recompute`. Segments cannot declare one. The `Permissions` base lives in
`@stackra/iam`, and `@stackra/iam` depends on `@stackra/segments`, so the import would close a package cycle, which
turbo refuses (BUILDER-RULES rule 6c). The owner asked on 2026-10-27 to settle this in the ADRs and to find every other
case like it.

The stackra-210 audit (`notes/handover/batches/permission-and-exemption-audit.md`) read core at `4a12b67`, OS `main`
at `72f073d8` and Commerce `commerce/wave-a` at `543d625`:

- Of 689 actions, 2 declare no permission. One is RecomputeSegments, the only one where a cycle is the cause. The other
  is OS `traffic.hit.record`, a public anonymous counter whose authors chose no permission; it is not a job.
- 66 actions have only Job or System actors. 65 declare a permission: 25 system-held (`Permission.Lx.system()`, "held
  only by the platform; no role may grant it") and 40 grantable.
- `@stackra/iam` reaches 37 core members, so none of them can declare a `Permissions` class. 10 of them sit below
  `@stackra/actions` and cannot declare an action at all. The other 26 can declare an action but not its permission.
  Only segments does today.
- The iam-to-segments edge is one type import: `SegmentRef`, in `@stackra/iam/contracts`' `Audience` type.
- At run time a call with no permission passes the permission step (`IamAccessControlDriver.require` returns at once),
  so for such an action the actor check of [0098](/adr/0098-actions-are-the-unit) decision 7 is the only gate.
- The same layering shape, a low package that cannot use a kind base above it, occurs in 9 more classes and one
  generated file: 7 `@stackra/contracts` drivers that cannot call `Driver(port)`, the `@stackra/config` and
  `@stackra/container` errors that cannot extend `Refusal`, and the `@stackra/testing` test setup. Lint accepts most of
  these through path exemptions in `stackra/base-matches-suffix`; gen refuses all of them. The audit finds 8 classes
  of exemption where the two disagree (STA-1365 items 1 to 4, 8 and 9).

## Decision

1. **A job-only action keeps a permission.** The two options:

   |          | Option A: gen accepts a Job- or System-only action without a permission                                                                                                                                                       | Option B: move the `Permissions` base below the packages that cannot reach iam                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                          |
   | -------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
   | Rule     | An action whose `actors` are only `Job` and `System` may have no permission; gen skips `permission_unknown` for it and records no permission in the manifest                                                                  | Every action has a permission, as now. A job's permission is system-held: `static readonly <verb> = Permission.Lx.system()`                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                             |
   | Change   | gen's `build-manifest`; a new source for the action's risk (today the risk comes from the permission, [0099](/adr/0099-risk-scale-l0-to-l4)), such as a `risk:` option; the audit, approval and budget steps learn to read it | Move `Permissions` (`core/bases/permissions.base.ts`), `Permission` (`core/values/permission.value.ts`) and `Risk` (`core/enums/risk.enum.ts`) from `@stackra/iam` to `@stackra/actions`; `@stackra/iam` re-exports all three from `.`, so no import changes. Their only imports are `registerKind` (`@stackra/container`) and `Enum` (`@stackra/contracts`), both below actions. gen matches kind bases by name in any `@stackra/*` package, so gen does not change. Segments then adds `SegmentPermissions` with `recompute = Permission.L1.system()` |
   | Reach    | Every one of the 66 Job/System-only actions may drop its permission: two ways to write a job                                                                                                                                  | The 26 packages above actions and below iam can declare permissions; one way to write a job                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                             |
   | Security | The only gate is the actor list; the call has no permission, no risk and no IAM record; a later actor added to the list (a `Service`, a `User`) silently gets an unguarded action unless gen refuses the combination          | Unchanged: the permission is system-held, no role can be granted it, the risk and audit stay as for every action                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                        |
   | Size     | gen rule and pipeline risk source, plus docs                                                                                                                                                                                  | 3 files moved, 3 re-exports, 1 new Permissions class in segments; lang keys for its label in all seven locales                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                          |

   **Recommendation: option B.** The case is 1 action of 689, while option A changes the rule for all 66 job-only
   actions and gives them a second form. 25 of them already use the system-held form option B asks for. Option A also
   removes the risk level of every action that uses it, and the risk feeds audit, approval and MCP hints (0099).
   Option B moves three files whose only dependencies sit below `@stackra/actions`. It fixes the whole class of case,
   not just segments: any package that can declare an action can then declare its permission. Today 26 packages could
   hit the same refusal on their first action, among them search, scheduler, outbox, settings and tenancy.

   A smaller fix exists, but it reaches segments only. Move the `SegmentRef` type out of `@stackra/segments`, into
   `@stackra/contracts` or into iam's own contracts. Then `@stackra/iam` no longer depends on segments, and segments
   can depend on iam, as webhooks, media and devices did in the STA-344 lanes. It may also be done beside option B,
   since a type-only edge that closes a cycle is worth removing anyway.

   Whichever option is chosen, gen's `permission_unknown` keeps refusing an action that has a user, agent, service
   or anonymous actor and no permission. The OS `traffic.hit.record` case stays a product refusal and takes a
   `Permission.L0` static.

   and why it holds. A placement that matches no row gets the ordinary kind rule (ADR 0294 R3) in both checks. A new
   row is added only by amending this ADR.

   the reason, so the disable comments go once lint reads the table. E2 and E3 cover all 19 `.error.ts` classes that
   extend `Error` today, so narrowing lint from "any class whose name ends in `Error`" to these two rows breaks nothing.
   If option B is chosen, E9 is not added.

3. **Amendments.** This record amends, and the pages it amends are not edited until it is Accepted:
   - [0098](/adr/0098-actions-are-the-unit): the planned gen check "rejects an action without a permission" holds for
     every action under option B; a job's permission is system-held. Under option A it gains the E9 exception.
   - [0132](/adr/0132-permissions-catalogue-and-policy-providers): under option B, the `Permissions` kind base,
     `Permission` and `Risk` live in `@stackra/actions`, and `@stackra/iam` re-exports them. The catalogue, the key
     form and the policy providers are unchanged.
   - [0294](/adr/0294-programming-model-v3-grammar-folders-identities-and-naming) R3 and decision 16: "a class extends
     exactly one kind base exported by a `@stackra/*` package" has the exemptions of decision 2, and no others.
   - [0003](/adr/0003-tier-dependency-direction): within `tier:lib`, a package that a kind base's package depends on
     uses the matching row of decision 2, never a new upward dependency.
   - [0232](/adr/0232-stackra-errors-package): `Refusal` is the error base everywhere except rows E2 and E3.
   - [0269](/adr/0269-stackra-testing-package-entries-and-helper-placement): row E4 is the harness rule, and row E8

   - [0099](/adr/0099-risk-scale-l0-to-l4): under option A only, an action without a permission takes its risk from a
     declared `risk:` option.

## Consequences

- With option B, every action has a permission and a risk. A job's permission is system-held, so no role can grant
  it, and the 40 job-only actions that use a grantable verb today stay valid. Whether they should move to `.system()`
  verbs is a product clean-up, not part of this record.
- With option A, gen and the pipeline gain a second job form and a new risk source, and every later change to an
  action's `actors` list needs a gen check that refuses a user, agent or service actor added to a permission-less
  action.
- gen and lint read one table, so a member that passes lint no longer fails gen on placement (STA-1365 items 1 to 4,
  8 and 9). The `oxlint-disable` comments in `container.service.ts` and `action-pipeline.service.ts` go away.
- Someone must keep the table: a new row is an amendment of this record, never a new path pattern in one checker.
- STA-1365 items 5 to 7 (`AreaStorageDriver`, `ProviderDrivers<…>` in documents, `I18nKey`) are gen reading gaps,
  not exemptions, and stay with STA-1365.

## Enforcement

- planned — STA-1365: gen honours rows E1 to E8 (and E9 under option A) and reads the same table as lint.
- planned — STA-1365 or a batch filed on acceptance: `stackra/base-matches-suffix`
  (`packages/developer/coding-standard/src/core/constants/base-matches-suffix-rule.constant.ts`) reads the table in
  place of its `BELOW_CONTAINER` and `TESTING_ENTRY` patterns and its "name ends in the kind" acceptance for errors.
  The home of the shared table must be a package both checks can import without a cycle (rule 6c).
- planned — on acceptance of option B: a batch moves `Permissions`, `Permission` and `Risk` into `@stackra/actions`
  with re-exports from `@stackra/iam`, and adds `SegmentPermissions`; gen's `permission_unknown` then passes over core.
- Nothing checks this record today; it is Proposed.
