## Context

[0203](/adr/0203-the-commercial-model-billing-provider-and-tier-names) put Figentra's own billing on Stripe Billing.
Stripe Billing was found unable to issue ZATCA or ETA invoices or charge mada and local wallets, and products need
subscriptions for their own customers (Academorix fees, Juristra retainers, Commerce subscription products). The owner
decided the billing and pricing rules in OS rounds 8 to 13 on 2026-10-25, and in round 22 that Stackra keeps only
the mechanism ([0259](/adr/0259-module-placement-three-homes)). The money hierarchy, hybrid pricing, plans, seats,
regional price tiers, discounts, support tiers and audit visibility per plan are Figentra's choices, recorded in Figentra
OS ADRs [0020](https://docs.os.figentra.com/adr/0020-one-os-billing-engine-for-figentra-plans-and-product-subscriptions)
to [0031](https://docs.os.figentra.com/adr/0031-how-tenants-pay-figentra-contracting-entities-and-tax).

## Decision

1. **The Stackra licence.** Stackra is sold to developers as the developer licence of
   [0202](/adr/0202-stackra-pro-is-developer-side-os-capability-is-free-code), issued and checked through Keygen
   ([0178](/adr/0178-licensing-keygen-licences-and-the-stackra-licensing-verifier)). It is the only commercial level
   Stackra itself owns; everything a product sells on top is the product's.
2. **One billing engine, the seller a parameter** (OS-59, OS-60). Subscriptions run on one engine whose seller is a
   parameter: the platform operator or a tenant. Plans, cycles, proration, dunning and invoice requests are the same
   for both. A due cycle emits an event that the product may turn into an order.
3. **Providers are drivers.** Payment providers (Stripe, Moyasar, Paymob and others) are drivers behind
   `@stackra/payments` ([0357](/adr/0357-payments-architecture-ports-gateway-drivers-a-platform-layer-and-a-closed-loop-wallet)).
   No vendor billing product is the system of record, and the engine never calls one.
4. **Built on Stackra's primitives.** The engine reads [0215](/adr/0215-entitlements) entitlements and
   [0216](/adr/0216-meters-and-usage-events) meters, and billing events create, renew and revoke Keygen licences
   (0178 decision 3). A seat meter counts the principal kinds the product declares as seats.

## Consequences

- One engine issues every subscription invoice request, including the fiscal ones a vendor billing product could not.
- Any product on Stackra can sell subscriptions to its own customers without a second engine.
- Each product owns its own price list, seat definition and plan ladder; Stackra ships none.

## Enforcement

planned — a dependency check that no vendor billing SDK (Stripe Billing, Chargebee and similar) is imported by the
billing engine; the seller-as-parameter contract test in the engine; the seat meter reading the product's declared seat
kinds.
