Better Stack runs on-call (ADR 0185 decision 1). The person on call is the first responder for every page and the
incident commander until they hand over ([Incident response](/runbooks/incident-response)).

## What pages

| Source                                                         | Declared in                                          | Pages as                                       |
| -------------------------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------- |
| An uptime monitor misses (a public host is down from outside)  | `observability.uptime` of the root `runway.yaml`     | A Better Stack incident: e-mail and push       |
| A heartbeat misses (a cron, an outbox relay or a queue worker) | `observability.heartbeats`, from each job's manifest | A Better Stack incident: e-mail and push       |
| A person declares an incident by hand                          | Better Stack, "Report an incident"                   | The escalation policy, like any incident       |
| A new or regressed error in an app                             | Sentry alert per deployed environment                | E-mail to the issue's owners; it does not page |

SEV1 pages at any hour. Anything that pages must need a person now; a signal that can wait for the morning is an
e-mail or a Linear issue, not a page.

## The escalation policy

The policy in Better Stack decides who hears about an unacknowledged incident, and when. Keep it this shape:

1. The person on call, by push and e-mail, then by phone call if the incident is not acknowledged.
2. The backup on-call person, when one exists.
3. The owner.

The waits between steps are set in Better Stack and recorded in the private runbook. Change them there, not here.

## When you are paged

<Steps>
  <Step title="Acknowledge">
    Acknowledge in the Better Stack app. That stops the escalation; it does not resolve anything.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Look">
    Open the monitor or heartbeat that fired and the logs for the same minutes (Better Stack,
    Workers Logs in Cloudflare, Sentry). Check whether a deploy went out just before.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Decide">
    If customers are affected, or could be, declare the incident and follow [Incident
    response](/runbooks/incident-response). If it was a false alarm, resolve it in Better Stack with
    one line saying why, and open a SEV4 issue to fix or remove the noisy check.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Get help early">
    Escalate by hand in Better Stack when the incident is beyond you or you cannot reach a laptop. A
    page you cannot work on is not a page you keep.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Before a shift

- The Better Stack app is installed, signed in, and allowed through Do Not Disturb for critical alerts.
- You can reach a laptop with access to GitHub, the cloud consoles, the deploy approvals and `figentra/secrets`
  ([Secrets](/runbooks/secrets)). Break-glass production access works for you and raises its alert
  ([ADR 0188](/adr/0188-operator-access-and-the-encryption-key-hierarchy) decision 1).
- You have read the last hand-over note.
- After a change to your phone or to the escalation policy, send yourself a test incident from Better Stack and
  acknowledge it.

## Hand-over

At the end of a shift write a hand-over note for the next person, as a Linear issue titled `On-call hand-over <date>`, with:

- incidents still open, with their level and the next step;
- deploys or migrations in flight;
- monitors that are paused or muted, and when they come back;
- anything that paged and was resolved as noise, with its SEV4 issue.

When the next person acknowledges the note, the open incidents are theirs.

## Keeping pages worth answering

- Every page needs an action. A page that needed none gets a SEV4 issue to tune or remove the check.
- A paused monitor has an end date. Pause it in Better Stack with a note, never by deleting it from `runway.yaml`.
- Review the month's pages once a month: what paged, at what hour, and whether it was real.

## What the owner sets up once

<Steps>
  <Step title="People and phones">
    In Better Stack, invite each on-call person, verify their phone for calls and push, and set
    their time zone.
  </Step>
  <Step title="The schedule">
    Create the on-call calendar. With one person, that person is on call all the time; add a
    rotation when a second person joins.
  </Step>
  <Step title="The escalation policy">
    Create the policy above and make it the default for the team, so every monitor and heartbeat
    that `apply-observability.yml` creates uses it.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Test it">
    Report a test incident and check that it reaches the person on call by push, then by phone.
  </Step>
</Steps>
