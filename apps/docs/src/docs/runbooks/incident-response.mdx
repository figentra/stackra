An incident is anything that hurts customers now or will soon: a service or the registry down, data lost or wrong,
a secret or personal data exposed. The order is always the same: **stop the harm, then find the cause, then make sure
it cannot happen the same way again.**

## Severity levels

Pick the level from the impact on customers, not from the cause. When in doubt, pick the higher level; lowering it later
costs nothing. Every incident, at every level, has a Linear issue and a postmortem
([ADR 0185](/adr/0185-incident-response-on-call-status-page-and-support-tiers) decision 2).

| Level    | Impact                                                                                                                                                                                                         | Who is told                                                                            | Linear priority |
| -------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | --------------- |
| **SEV1** | Production is down or unsafe for many customers: a production service or the registry is unavailable, data is lost or corrupted, personal data is exposed, a production secret leaked and is still valid.      | Better Stack pages the on-call person at any hour. Status page and affected customers. | Urgent          |
| **SEV2** | A major function is broken or badly degraded for some customers with no workaround, or a SEV1 is likely if nothing is done (backups failing, a critical heartbeat missing, an exposed secret already revoked). | The on-call person at once, by push and e-mail. Status page when customers can see it. | High            |
| **SEV3** | A minor function is broken or slow and a workaround exists, or internal delivery is blocked (the gate red on `main`, a release stuck).                                                                         | The Linear issue; worked the next business day.                                        | Medium          |
| **SEV4** | No customer impact: a cosmetic fault, a noisy monitor, a near miss worth learning from.                                                                                                                        | The Linear issue; planned like other work.                                             | Low             |

- A security or personal-data incident starts at SEV2 or higher and stays there until it is triaged.
- The level can change while the incident runs. Write every change on the timeline with the time and the reason.

## Roles

The team is small, so one person may hold every role. Name who holds each one on the issue anyway, so a second person
who joins knows who decides.

| Role                   | Does                                                                                                                                                                                                     |
| ---------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Incident commander** | Owns the incident until it is handed over in writing. Decides the level, the mitigation and when it is resolved. Starts as the on-call person who acknowledged the page.                                 |
| **Operator**           | Runs the commands: rollback, deploy, rotation, restore. Production deploys and rollbacks are the owner's ([ADR 0170](/adr/0170-deployment-what-deploys-where-and-how-a-release-is-promoted) decision 8). |
| **Communications**     | Writes the status page updates and the customer messages. The incident commander until someone else takes it.                                                                                            |
| **Scribe**             | Keeps the timeline. The `incident-commander` agent can do this and draft the postmortem; it writes documents only and never runs a mitigation or reads a secret.                                         |

## The response

<Steps>
  <Step title="Acknowledge">
    Acknowledge the page in Better Stack before anything else. An unacknowledged page escalates to the
    next person on the escalation policy ([On-call](/runbooks/on-call)).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Declare">
    Open a Linear issue titled `SEV<n>: <what customers see>`, set its priority from the table above
    and start the timeline in it: UTC times, one line per event, each with who acted. The first line
    is when the problem was detected and by what (a monitor, a heartbeat, Sentry, a customer).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Stop the harm">
    Mitigate before you diagnose. In order of preference:

    1. **Roll back** the last deploy if the incident started after it: `stackra runway rollback <sha>`
       redeploys the last good release within 15 minutes ([Deploys: Rollback](/runbooks/deploys#rollback),
       [runway rollback](/runway/rollback)). A release whose migration contracted the schema is rolled
       forward instead.
    2. **Switch the feature off** with its setting or flag, if it has one.
    3. **Revoke** a leaked credential at its vendor ([Secrets: A value was exposed](/runbooks/secrets#a-value-was-exposed)).
    4. **Restore** lost or corrupted data ([Disaster recovery](/runbooks/disaster-recovery)).

    Fix forward under pressure only when none of these is possible, and write down why.

  </Step>
  <Step title="Tell people">
    Update the status page (once it is published) when the incident is declared and at every state
    change: investigating, identified, monitoring, resolved. Say what customers see and what they
    can do; never the cause before it is known, a customer's name or any personal data. Post the same
    update on the Linear issue.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Find the cause">
    Work from evidence: the Better Stack incident and logs, Workers Logs in Cloudflare, Sentry, the
    traces, the deploy records (GitHub Deployments) and `git log` of the deployed commit. An
    unexplained change is a question to answer, not a conclusion about who made it.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Resolve">
    Resolve when customers are no longer affected and the monitors that fired are green again. Close
    the incident in Better Stack and on the status page. Leave the Linear issue open: it closes when
    the postmortem is written and its actions are filed.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Write the postmortem">
    Within five business days of resolving, at every level ([Postmortem](/runbooks/postmortem)).
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Telling customers

The support terms are Figentra's and live in [OS ADR 0062](https://docs.os.figentra.com/adr/0062-operations-slas-and-support-tiers)
(summarised in the Note of ADR 0185): the next business day by e-mail for the entry tier, 8 business hours for the mid
tier and a 4-hour urgent response for Enterprise. There is no 24/7 cover in any contract until a second on-call person
exists. Community support is GitHub Discussions (ADR 0185 decision 6).

- Answer inside the customer's tier, even when the answer is only "we are on it, next update at a stated time".
- Promise a next update, never a fix time.
- An Enterprise customer affected by a SEV1 or SEV2 hears from us directly, not only through the status page.

## Security incidents

A leaked secret, an unexpected operator session, a suspected intrusion. Everything above applies, plus:

- **A leaked secret** is revoked at the vendor first, then rotated ([Secrets: A value was exposed](/runbooks/secrets#a-value-was-exposed),
  [Token rotation after exposure](/runbooks/token-rotation)). Rewriting git history does not un-leak it. Report
  scanner hits by rule id and path only, never the value.
- **An unexpected break-glass alert** is a SEV1 until it is explained: operator access to production is break-glass
  only, and every session raises an alert ([ADR 0188](/adr/0188-operator-access-and-the-encryption-key-hierarchy)
  decision 1).
- **Keep the evidence.** Do not delete logs, rotate away an attacker's session record or rebuild a host before the
  audit trail is copied. The audit log is hash-chained ([ADR 0212](/adr/0212-audit-evidence-access-logs-hash-chain-and-retention)).
- **Keep it private.** The Linear issue holds the detail; the status page says only what customers need to act on.

## Personal-data breaches

A breach is personal data lost, changed, disclosed or made unavailable without authority. GDPR, the UAE and KSA PDPL
and the children's data rules apply ([ADR 0189](/adr/0189-privacy-operations-and-data-retention) decision 6), and
their notification clocks start when we become aware, not when the cause is found.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Record the moment of awareness">
    Write the UTC time on the issue. Under GDPR a controller notifies its supervisory authority
    within 72 hours of awareness when the breach is likely to put people at risk (Article 33), and a
    processor tells each controller without undue delay. For a tenant's data we are usually the
    processor and the tenant is the controller.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Scope it">
    Which tenants, which field classes ([ADR
    0134](/adr/0134-data-invariants-classification-and-delete-lifecycle)), how many people, whether
    any are children, and whether the data was encrypted with a key the attacker did not have.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Contain it">
    Close the path, revoke the credentials it used, and keep the evidence as above.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Decide on notification (owner)">
    The owner decides, with counsel, who is notified and when: the affected tenants, the supervisory
    authorities and, where the risk to people is high, the people themselves (GDPR Article 34). The
    UAE and KSA laws have their own duties, which counsel confirms for the case. Agents never
    contact a regulator, a tenant or a data subject.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Record it">
    Every breach is recorded with its facts, effects and remedy, even one that needs no
    notification. The record stays in the private issue.
  </Step>
</Steps>
