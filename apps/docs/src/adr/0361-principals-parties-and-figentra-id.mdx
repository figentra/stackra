## Context

Three things were mixed up in the OS planning: who can sign in, who can act, and who a tenant does business with. The
OS draft had a `people` service owning a tenant-scoped Person (OS-351) and Company (OS-352), identity owning the login,
and devices in identity ([0235](/adr/0235-devices-and-iot-minimal-now-full-later) decision 1) while the Commerce answer
33 put them in an OS devices service (OS-43). Shoppers needed a sign-in that works across merchants without merchants
seeing each other's data (OS-25), and residency rules put sign-in identifiers inside each region (OS-22). The owner
asked how bots, AI agents and integrations fit, and whether to rename `people`. The owner answered in chat on
2026-10-25 (OS rounds 3, 5 and 7).

## Decision

1. **Figentra ID is login only.** Figentra ID is the one login layer for every Figentra product, on ZITADEL, with one
   ZITADEL instance per region (`eu`, `me`, `us`). It holds credentials, sign-in methods and sessions. It holds no business
   record about a person.
2. **Principals live in identity and IAM.** Every actor that can authenticate or be granted access is a principal in
   the identity service and IAM: humans, AI agents, bots, service accounts, API keys, OAuth apps, devices and
   integrations. Grants, roles and approvals apply to principals only.
3. **Parties hold the tenant's records of people and organisations.** The OS `people` service is renamed `parties`.
   Per tenant it holds `Person` and `Organisation` records: customers, clients, students, leads, suppliers and
   companies. The console label may read "Contacts". A party is not a principal.
4. **A party links to a login only by sign-up or consent.** A `Person` links to a Figentra ID account only when the
   human signs up, or consents to the link. A party without a login is normal.
5. **Products add their own fields.** Products extend parties with their own fields and records, never with a second
   person or organisation model: Commerce B2B on organisations, Juristra clients, Academorix students.
6. **Shoppers sign in once.** An end customer signs in with Figentra ID, shown in the product's brand. Each merchant
   holds its own customer profile (a party) linked to that login only by sign-up or consent. Merchants never see each
   other's data or each other's customer profiles.
7. **Devices are a separate OS service.** The OS `devices` service holds device inventory, pairing, health and
   location. Identity issues and revokes device credentials; a device is still a principal (decision 2). This
   replaces the "device registry in identity" of 0235 decision 1. A device registry and pairing port in
   `@stackra/peripherals` fronts that service, and every product shares it (Commerce answer 33).
8. **Support access** (OS-49, OS-50). Figentra support reads a tenant by default. A write needs the tenant admin's
   consent each time, lasts 15 minutes and is audited. An admin may pre-approve set days, and Enterprise may extend or
   switch support access off. This changes the limits of [0211](/adr/0211-memberships-and-support-access) decision 3.
9. **Every sign-in method at launch** (OS-33, OS-35): email, passkeys, phone code, Google, Apple, Microsoft, Nafath,
   UAE Pass and Egypt digital ID. The government sign-in contracts become launch blockers.
10. **Login codes and identity checks are metered** to the tenant, delivered by WhatsApp first with SMS as fallback,
    per market (OS-36, OS-57).
11. **Till staff** (Commerce answers 31 and 32): PIN quick-switch and a manager override above limits live in Stackra
    identity and IAM, at launch. A PIN-only cashier is a principal but not a seat
    ([0358](/adr/0358-money-plans-and-pricing-four-levels-one-billing-engine-and-hybrid-pricing) decision 8).

## Consequences

- One question has one home: "can it sign in" is Figentra ID, "may it do this" is IAM, "who is this customer to us" is
  parties.
- A shopper with accounts at two merchants has one login and two separate profiles, so a merchant's export, erasure or
  consent touches only its own profile.
- Every product's customer, client or student model becomes a reference to a party plus its own fields; product
  migrations must map their own person tables onto parties.
- Revoking a device stays one revocation path in identity, while inventory and pairing move out of identity.
- The OS catalogue renames `people` (or `people-activity`) to `parties` and adds `devices`.

## Enforcement

planned — the OS catalogue check: no service other than `parties` declares a person or organisation model, and no
service other than identity issues credentials. planned — a conformance test in the identity service that a revoked
device credential is refused (as 0235 already plans). Nothing checks these rules yet.

## Open questions

- **Staff records.** The principals report lists a staff or employee record separate from the user account (I5),
  owned by OS workforce or people. Whether staff are parties, workforce records, or both, is not decided.
- **Activity.** The OS draft's `people-activity` service also held the cross-product timeline (OS-228). Whether the
  timeline moves with `parties` or stays elsewhere is not decided.
- **Staff accounts per region.** [0206](/adr/0206-zitadel-is-the-identity-provider-and-stackra-identity-is-provider-neutral)
  decision 5 says Staff have one global account. With one ZITADEL instance per region, whether staff keep one account
  per region or a global one is not decided.
- **Where the registry port lives.** The owner's answer puts the device registry port in `@stackra/peripherals`;
  [0235](/adr/0235-devices-and-iot-minimal-now-full-later) decision 2 names a `@stackra/devices` package. Whether the
  two are one package is not decided.
- **Which repository records this.** The Figentra OS repository holds uncommitted ADRs on principals, parties,
  Figentra ID, sign-in methods and support access. Which repository keeps which decision is open; see
  [0358](/adr/0358-money-plans-and-pricing-four-levels-one-billing-engine-and-hybrid-pricing) open question 5.
- **Directory details** (ZITADEL Cloud or self-hosted per region, one account per region, the region picker) are
  recorded in [0353](/adr/0353-data-residency-three-regions-fixed-at-sign-up-a-global-login-directory-and-copies-within-a-jurisdiction)
  from the defaults OS-22 and OS-24, not here.
- **Customer realm.** [0206](/adr/0206-zitadel-is-the-identity-provider-and-stackra-identity-is-provider-neutral)
  decision 5 gave each tenant a ZITADEL org for its end customers. How the customer realm and per-tenant login
  branding map onto one regional Figentra ID directory is not designed yet.
