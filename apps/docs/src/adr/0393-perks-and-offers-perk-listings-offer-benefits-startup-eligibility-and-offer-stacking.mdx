## Context

The owner added a partner perks page (partners pay to be listed) and a startup programme (RV-52: incubator and
accelerator members from an editable list, plus software startups, get 50% and AI credits for 12 months). Review round
3 (M3-6, M3-7, R3-SO-18) found no perk record, no non-price benefits on an offer and no stacking rule: Founding,
startup, Switcher and annual discounts together could reach zero.

## Decision

1. **Perk listings.** `@stackra/registry` adds the listing role `perk` beside `app`, `connector` and `agency` (listing
   roles, not the Integration roles of 0355). A perk never installs. It is redeemed by `code`, `link` or `lead`; a lead
   sends only the listing's named fields and only after the tenant admin consents (US-62). It is shown when approved
   and listed for the tenant's pack and product. Its listing fee is the flat fee of its `fee-schedule:<name>` in force
   on the period start, invoiced by OS billing to the partner's developer tenant (`perkListingFee`); no schedule means a
   free listing.
2. **Offer benefits.** An offer holds `benefits[]` of four kinds: `percent` (basis points), `credit_grant` (credit
   kind, units, period), `free_migration` and `module_grant`, each but the migration for a number of months or while
   the offer holds; and `eligibility` (packs, products, a rule-set key, a cap per pack). `applyOffers` gives one
   month's price and grants.
3. **Startup eligibility** is `rule-set:os:startup-perk-eligibility`, seeded in the `RuleSetPack$` shape: incubator
   members, or software companies incorporated in the last 730 days. The incubator list is the editable
   `settings:os:startup-incubators` record (shipped empty); OS billing sets the fact
   `organisation.incubator_member` from it. The 50% and AI credits are `offer:startup` rows, not rule outcomes.
4. **Stacking** is `rule-set:os:offer-stacking`: an offer the rule matches stacks; of the rest, the single best
   (largest percentage) applies and the others are refused and reported (`stackOffers`). The seed lets only
   `offer:annual` stack.
5. **No new dependency.** `@stackra/registry` and `@stackra/pricing` do not import `@stackra/rules`; OS billing runs
   `decide` and passes the answers in.

## Consequences

Perks, offers, eligibility and stacking are edited in the admin. OS billing owns the perk-redemption workflow, the
consent log and the facts the rule sets read.

## Enforcement

Unit tests of the perk functions and schema, the offer functions, and a data test of the seeds
(`packages/application/registry/src/core/__tests__/offer-seeds.test.ts`). planned — seeding checks the rule sets
against the OS consumer's `Rules`.
