## Context

[0203](/adr/0203-the-commercial-model-billing-provider-and-tier-names) put Figentra's own billing on Stripe Billing.
[0242](/adr/0242-commercial-model-plan-ladder-add-ons-metric-and-trial) set a ladder per product with shared add-ons
and a hybrid metric. Since then the owner asked for one clear hierarchy from the Stackra licence down to a tenant's own
customers, Stripe Billing was found unable to issue ZATCA or ETA invoices or charge mada and local wallets, and products
need subscriptions for their own customers (Academorix fees, Juristra retainers, Commerce subscription products). The
owner decided the hierarchy (Commerce questionnaire Answers) and the billing and pricing rules in OS rounds 8 to 13, on
2026-10-25.

## Decision

**The hierarchy.**

1. Money has four levels:
   1. the **Stackra licence**, the developer licence ([0202](/adr/0202-stackra-pro-is-developer-side-os-capability-is-free-code));
   2. **Figentra OS plans**, billed by one billing engine;
   3. **one plan per product**, which bundles the product's OS part and, where it applies, its Commerce part;
   4. **the tenant's own customers**, who pay through Figentra Pay in the product's brand
      ([0357](/adr/0357-payments-architecture-one-engine-gateway-drivers-a-platform-layer-and-figentra-pay)).

**One billing engine (OS-59, OS-60).**

2. The OS billing engine is the system of record for Figentra's own plans and for every product's customer
   subscriptions. The seller is a parameter: Figentra or a tenant. Plans, cycles, dunning and invoices are the same for
   both. Stripe, Moyasar and Paymob are payment drivers behind `@stackra/payments`, not the billing system.
3. No paying customer is taken until OS billing is built; there are no manual-invoice pilots (OS-13, OS-14).
4. Tenants pay Figentra by card, mada included; by bank transfer for large annual plans; Enterprise on net-30. In Egypt
   also by Paymob cards, Fawry reference codes, InstaPay or bank transfer (OS-85, OS-384).

**Hybrid pricing (round 9).**

5. Every product is priced the same way:
   - a **base plan** per product, Starter to Enterprise, with the core included;
   - **paid first-party modules** in the app market
     ([0355](/adr/0355-marketplace-one-integration-with-up-to-five-roles-one-app-market-and-open-to-any-developer));
   - **usage credits** for messages, AI and KYC;
   - an **Enterprise annual licence per module**: dedicated, regional or on-prem through Keygen
     ([0178](/adr/0178-licensing-keygen-licences-and-the-stackra-licensing-verifier));
   - **Figentra Pay fees** ([0357](/adr/0357-payments-architecture-one-engine-gateway-drivers-a-platform-layer-and-figentra-pay)).
6. **Feature map.** Every product's questionnaire classifies each feature as core, tier or module.
7. **Figentra OS sold alone** (OS-61, OS-62): its own Starter to Enterprise ladder with priced app add-ons, on sale
   after Commerce release 1. The draft split (OS-63, OS-380): core is tasks and approvals, collaboration, data exchange,
   translation, the assistant, and the apps a product depends on; modules are marketing, workforce, help desk, CRM, AI
   agents and scheduling.

**Seats and limits.**

8. **Seats** (OS-67, OS-68) are active internal members only. Pending invites, suspended users, service accounts, AI
   agents, guests, support sessions and PIN-only till staff are not seats. Adding a seat is charged prorated at once;
   removing one takes effect at renewal.
9. **Across products** (OS-64, OS-77): seats count per product. Storage, AI credits and automation runs are pooled per
   organisation, with optional caps per child tenant.
10. **Packs and overage** (OS-73, OS-135): prepaid packs for AI, messages and automation runs, with auto top-up and a
    monthly cap. API calls over the included amount are billed on Business and above; smaller tiers are blocked until
    they upgrade.
11. **Messages** (OS-74, OS-394): credits with per-country multipliers, charged per SMS segment, with the cost shown
    before sending. Bringing one's own provider is in
    [0356](/adr/0356-choosing-a-provider-per-tenant-and-market-through-the-connection-with-a-market-default) decision 5.
12. **Automations** (OS-229): the automation builder is in every plan, limited by active automations and metered runs,
    with top-ups.

**Prices.**

13. **Regional price tiers by billing country** (OS-66, OS-383): high (UAE, KSA, Qatar, Kuwait, EU, US), standard
    (Bahrain, Oman, Jordan and similar) and low (Egypt and similar). Each tier is a local-currency price list (SAR,
    AED, EGP, EUR, USD). The billing address, checked against the card country or tax registration, sets the tier; the
    IP address only shows an estimate. EGP prices are reviewed quarterly with 30 days' notice. Annual plans are fixed for
    their term. Merchants get the same feature through pricing's Markets ([0244](/adr/0244-markets-owned-by-pricing)).
14. **Price and limit changes** apply at the next renewal, with notice (OS-82).
15. **Discount programmes are fixed** (OS-381): annual discount; nonprofit and education 30%; startups 50% for the first
    year. Each is a dated price list. No ad-hoc coupon without a second approver.
16. **Setting the numbers** (OS-378, OS-379): a cost per tenant per service is built first; the owner then approves the
    whole limit and price table once, benchmarked against Zoho, Odoo, Salla and Zid, and reviews it every 6 months.
    Until then the numbers of the 0242 ladder are placeholders.

**Settled by their recommended default (owner rule, `default-applied.md`).**

17. The plan catalogue is authored once and replicated read-only to each region; billing runs in the organisation's
    home region (OS-65). Upgrades apply at once with a prorated charge; downgrades at period end (OS-80). The 14-day
    trial is one per product per organisation, and sales may extend it (OS-83). Figentra's own invoices go through OS
    invoicing as tenant zero (OS-86). Overrides need a reason and an expiry, and four-eyes above a threshold (OS-87).
    Product operators other than Figentra do not bill through OS at launch; the seller party stays in the model (OS-89).
    Custom projects are billed as one-off quotes and milestone invoices in the same engine (OS-90).

**Devices and external users.**

18. **Devices and external users** (OS-45, OS-68, OS-32). Devices count against a device limit per plan (placeholder
    2, 10, 60 and custom); peripherals do not count; PIN-only till staff are not seats. The offline limit is 1 to 72
    hours, default 24. External users (clients, outside counsel, B2B buyers) ship at launch and are free up to a
    fair-use cap per plan.

**Support and audit.**

19. **Support tiers and uptime** (OS-252, OS-300, OS-301). The entry tier gets email support the next day, the mid tier
    a response in 8 business hours, and Enterprise a 4-hour response to urgent issues. The target is 99.5% without
    credits on Starter and Pro, and 99.9% with credits on Business and up. There is no 24/7 cover until a second
    on-call person exists.
20. **Audit** (OS-285, OS-286, OS-287). Figentra keeps every audit record for the period of
    [0212](/adr/0212-audit-evidence-access-logs-hash-chain-and-retention). Plans limit only what a tenant can see,
    every tier can search and export, and a paid add-on gives longer history, the API, a SIEM stream and proofs.

## Consequences

- One engine issues every invoice and runs every subscription, including the fiscal ones Stripe Billing could not.
- A price page lists a base plan, modules, credits and Pay fees for every product in the same shape.
- No revenue before OS billing ships: billing is on the critical path of the first launch.
- Every product owes a feature map (core, tier, module) before its plan is priced.
- The cost model and the owner's one-time approval of the table come before any price is published.

## Enforcement

planned — the billing engine with the seller as a parameter in OS billing; the entitlement table test of 0242 extended
to the price tiers and packs; the seat meter excluding the listed principal kinds; the second-approver check on
coupons and discount price lists; a check that every product's catalogue classifies each feature as core, tier or
module.

## Open questions

1. **Annual discount size.** OS-66's default said "annual is 2 months free"; the owner's answer names an annual
   discount (OS-381) without the size.
2. **Country lists of the price tiers** are set with the cost model, not here (OS-66).
3. **Entities and tax** (OS-8, OS-385, OS-419: one UAE company under DIFC law for every me customer and one EU company;
   VAT KSA 15%, UAE 5%, Egypt 14%, EU reverse charge; counsel confirms) are recorded in the owner answers but belong to a
   legal ADR, not this one.
4. **Other 0242 changes not made here.** The sandbox count per tier (OS-16) is in
   [0359](/adr/0359-modes-and-configuration-environments-display-modes-sandbox-promotion-and-live-records). The device
   limit and external users (decision 18), support (decision 19) and audit (decision 20) also change the ladder; the
   ladder's numbers stay placeholders.
5. **Which repository records which decision.** The same owner answers are recorded twice: in Stackra ADRs 0353 to
   0361 and in uncommitted ADRs of the Figentra OS repository (for example its 0001 to 0068, which also claim to amend
   Stackra 0171 and 0172 and leave open whether KSA moves to AWS and where Egypt is hosted). One home per decision is
   not chosen. Suggested split, for the owner to confirm: Stackra keeps framework rules (ports, gen checks, the plan
   gate, data-model rules) and the Notes on Stackra ADRs; Figentra OS keeps OS-service and business rules (the billing
   engine, the payments service, parties and devices, prices, marketplace terms). A duplicate then becomes a link by
   URL, never a bare number from another repository.
6. **Support tier names.** The owner's support answer (decision 19) names an entry tier, a mid tier and Enterprise,
   not which plans they are.
