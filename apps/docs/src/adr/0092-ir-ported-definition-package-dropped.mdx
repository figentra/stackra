---
title: "0092 — The IR is ported with decorator-derived kinds; the definition package is dropped"
description: "Proposed · 2026-10-19"
---

## Context

Figentra OS's application category holds six packages. Only `definition` and `ir` are real; `compiler`, `schema-gen`,
`runtime-core` and `experience` are stubs. `definition` declares six kinds through `define*()` functions and YAML. Stackra
authors everything with decorators, and codegen reads decorator metadata through the container
([0090](/adr/0090-codegen-discovery-by-root-module)). It still needs a stable, versioned intermediate representation between
discovery and emitters, and the `ir` package already provides one.

## Decision

1. **The `ir` machinery is ported**: the `v: 1` envelope, deterministic serialise and parse, cycle detection, the
   compatibility rule, and `SchemaIR` with `schemaIRToZod`.
2. **IR kinds are derived from decorators**: model, endpoint, event, job, cron, resource and permission. They replace the six
   definition kinds.
3. **The `definition` package is dropped**, including `define*()` and YAML definitions.
4. **The remaining application stubs** (`compiler`, `schema-gen`, `runtime-core`, `experience`) are not ported as they are.
   Codegen ADRs decide what replaces them.

## Consequences

- One serialised form feeds every emitter (Encore app, wire types, Zod, docs), and its compatibility rule makes IR changes reviewable.
- No second authoring path: a kind that cannot be expressed as a decorator does not exist.
- [0084](/adr/0084-figentra-os-package-port-plan) records `ir` as port + adapt and `definition` as skip.

## Enforcement

Planned: the ported round-trip and cycle-detection tests in the IR package's `__tests__`, and a compatibility test that fails
an IR change without a version bump. Nothing checks this today.

## Supersedes and sources

Supersedes: none. Narrows [0018](/adr/0018-framework-design-record) decision 3: the IR becomes in scope for Stackra, while
product compilers stay out. Sources: owner decision in chat, 2026-10-19 (Round 9 G4 and the source survey).
