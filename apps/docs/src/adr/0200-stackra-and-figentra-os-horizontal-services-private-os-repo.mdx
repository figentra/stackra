## Context

Stackra ships packages, and Figentra OS ships services built on them. [0031](/adr/0031-coarse-services-split-on-evidence) (Proposed) assumed coarse services split on evidence, which the 36-service catalogue
of the owner's decisions replaces. The owner's answers FO1, FO5 to FO9 (mem L501, L510, L513, 2026-10-20) fix the boundary.

## Decision

1. **Horizontal services (FO1).** Business services are built on Stackra and consumed by products. Each can also be sold on its own.
2. **Private OS repository (FO5 and FO7).** The OS lives in a private `figentra/figentra-os` repository, with `services/<name>`, `apps/console` and `runway.yaml`. Local packages are linked through pnpm overrides for co-development,
   and CI uses released versions. One Claude session works across both repositories, and the OS `AGENTS.md` inherits Stackra's rules.
3. **Built on Stackra from day one (FO6, FO8 and FO9).** Every OS service uses Stackra packages. There is one platform account and tenant across products, with per-product entitlements. SaaS comes first,
   and on-prem and air-gap are built and tested from day one.
4. **Catalogue.** The service catalogue is `svc`, plus pricing (contradiction C-30).
5. **Sizing.** Service sizing becomes topology (ADR 0169), not a split decision.

## Consequences

- A horizontal service can be sold alone, which makes its entitlements part of its own contract.
- Co-development needs the pnpm overrides, so a broken local package shows up in the OS repository before it is released.

## Enforcement

planned — the OS repository's CI rejects a `workspace:` link to a package that is not released, except in the co-development profile.
