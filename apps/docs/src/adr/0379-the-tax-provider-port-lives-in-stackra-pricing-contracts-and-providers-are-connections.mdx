## Context

Wave 10 put a `TaxProvider` port in `@stackra/pricing/contracts`, a rate-table engine (`taxByRates`) and a `memory`
driver; the US sales-tax provider driver was not built, and the row was marked "new, not filed". A container port is
bound once per deployment, but which tax engine serves a sale depends on the market and, under "managed or bring your
own", on the tenant: the 2026-10-26 rounds set Stripe Tax as the managed default and Avalara as an add-on, with a
tenant's own account allowed (US-46, M3-4, R3-SO-19).

## Decision

1. **Home.** `TaxProvider` stays in `@stackra/pricing/contracts`, because tax is computed on the Market and pricing
   owns Markets (0244). No new package and no separate tax package.
2. **Two ways to answer.** A market whose own tables suffice (VAT and GST markets) answers with `taxByRates` over the
   pack's rate table. A market that needs a provider (US sales tax) answers through a provider. Which one is pack data:
   `market-pack:<code>.tax.engine` (`rates` or `provider`).
3. **Providers are Connections.** A tax provider is an Integration whose Connection implements a `TaxGateway`
   interface (quote, and record and void a finalised transaction where the provider keeps one), in
   `@stackra/pricing/contracts`. The production `TaxProvider` driver resolves the Connection through `ProviderResolver`
   under the `tax` capability, so a tenant's own account wins and the managed Connection serves otherwise. Provider
   drivers live with the integrations connectors, not in pricing.
4. **First providers.** Stripe Tax is the managed default where a pack's engine is `provider`; Avalara is an add-on and
   an own-account option. Both are pack and catalogue data (`market-pack:us.providers.tax`, `provider-category:tax`),
   never code.
5. **Snapshot on the document.** The quote, the provider and its transaction reference are stored on the order or
   invoice, so a reprint or a refund never recomputes tax with today's rates.
6. **A missing rate is an error.** As built: a line whose class has no rate refuses; it is never taxed at zero.
7. **Values.** Pack data: rate tables, tax engine per market, tax providers, registration thresholds. Catalogue data:
   `provider-category:tax` modes and the add-on price. Settings: the tenant's tax registrations and product tax
   classes. Rule sets: tax per line exceptions (config-model section 4). Code: the port, `taxByRates` and the drivers.

## Consequences

- A VAT market needs no provider and no network call to price a sale.
- A US tenant can bring its own Avalara account without a release.
- Finalising and refunding an invoice in a provider market adds a provider call, which the invoicing workflow makes.

## Enforcement

planned — `@stackra/pricing` tests: the driver picks `taxByRates` or the resolved Connection from pack data only, and a
missing rate refuses.

## Open questions

1. **Record and void timing.** Whether recording happens at invoice finalisation or at payment capture is the OS
   invoicing workflow's choice and is not decided here.
