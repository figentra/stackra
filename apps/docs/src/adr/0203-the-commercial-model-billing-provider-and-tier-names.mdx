## Context

The owner's commercial answers (mem L515, L526, L947–952, 2026-10-20) fix how Figentra bills its own customers. An earlier answer listed a Free tier, and the later answer (Start FINAL, L952) removed it.

## Decision

1. **Billing provider (PP1).** Stripe Billing, with Stripe Tax and the customer portal, bills Figentra's own customers. Its webhooks drive Keygen and the entitlements (ADR 0178). Enterprise and on-prem deals use annual invoices.
2. **Tiers.** Starter, Pro, Business and Enterprise (L952). There is no Free plan. The numbers of each tier are in ADR 0242.
3. **Add-ons and usage.** Paid module add-ons per service, and metered usage on top. Entitlements are the plan, plus add-ons, plus overrides.
4. **Metric.** Hybrid: a platform fee per tier, plus seats for internal users, plus metered usage for cost drivers (ADR 0242). A tenant's end customers are never seats.
5. **Developer licence.** Stackra Pro is a separate per-organisation developer licence (ADR 0202).

## Consequences

- A paying customer's plan lives in one billing provider, and every entitlement is derived from it.
- The tier names match the ladder in ADR 0242, so a price page cannot list a tier the product does not have.

## Enforcement

planned — the billing webhook contract test (ADR 0178) and the tier-name check in the pricing package.
