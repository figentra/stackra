## Context

Stackra's security target includes GDPR practices, and products that install it hold personal data about their tenants' users. Per-model PII field encryption and
archive, soft or hard delete policies are planned opt-ins in the data model. Apple requires a privacy manifest and App Tracking Transparency handling for iOS apps.

## Decision

1. **Data export and erasure are framework capabilities**: a subject's data can be exported and erased through one audited operation per service that owns personal data. Erasure follows each model's delete policy (archive, soft, hard).
2. **Personal data is declared per model** (the PII opt-in), so export and erasure can enumerate it.
3. **iOS apps ship a privacy manifest** listing collected data and required-reason API use. Dependencies' manifests are checked at build.
4. **ATT**: tracking is off by default; a product that enables it shows the ATT prompt and says why in the manifest.
5. **Templates for a privacy policy and a data-processing agreement** live in the docs for products to adapt. They are templates, not legal advice; legal review is each product's.
6. **Logs, error reports and analytics carry no personal data** ([0020](/adr/0020-observability-and-crash-reporting) rule 3, [0073](/adr/0073-observability-better-stack-sentry-encore)).

## Consequences

- Every model holding PII has more required metadata.
- Erasure from backups follows the point-in-time retention window; the statement made to users is `TODO`.

## Enforcement

Planned: a check that models flagged PII are covered by export and erasure tests; a build step validating the iOS privacy manifest. Nothing exists yet.
