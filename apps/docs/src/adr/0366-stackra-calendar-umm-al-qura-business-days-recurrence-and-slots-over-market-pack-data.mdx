## Context

OS scheduling-calendar, geography and workforce, Commerce (delivery promise, Hijri promotions), Muslin (seasons),
Beautilon (recurring appointments, processing gaps), Juristra (deadlines, court calendars) and Academorix (recurring
sessions, age cut-offs) all need the same date mechanism, and nothing is on disk. Each country's working week, public
holidays, Hijri adjustments and court recesses differ and change by announcement, and moon-sighted holidays are not
known in advance. [0363](/adr/0363-configuration-over-code-business-behaviour-is-data-rules-workflows-and-studios) forbids any of that in code. The same arithmetic must run offline on a till and on the server.
Alternatives weighed: a third-party RRULE and time-zone stack (`rrule`, `luxon`), and a Hijri table shipped in the
package; both add dependencies or data the runtime's ICU already provides.

## Decision

1. **Package.** `@stackra/calendar` in `packages/foundation/calendar`, `tier:lib`, entry `.` only, depending on
   `@stackra/contracts` and `zod`. It is pure mechanism with no persistence, so it sits in `foundation`, not `data`.
2. **One storage form.** Dates are stored and passed as Gregorian `YYYY-MM-DD`; instants as ISO 8601 with an offset.
   Hijri is computed with the runtime's `islamic-umalqura` calendar (`hijriOf`, `gregorianOf`). A runtime without it
   throws; the package never falls back to the arithmetic Hijri calendar.
3. **Calendar data, not code.** A `BusinessCalendar` holds a time zone, a working week (weekdays with hours; a span
   whose end is not after its start runs past midnight), holiday rules and `uncertainDays`. A holiday rule is a dated
   span, a yearly Gregorian date or a yearly Hijri date with `sighted`, `since` and `until`. Market packs supply the
   base; tenants, branches and courts add closures and recesses as more rules. A court working week is the same data.
   `BusinessCalendar$` checks the shape; the package ships no values.
4. **Uncertain, never guessed.** A sighted Hijri occurrence is `uncertain` until a dated rule with the same key in that
   Hijri year confirms or moves it. Business-day results list every uncertain day crossed. Whether an uncertain day
   counts as working or closed is the calendar's `uncertainDays` value.
5. **Time zones and working hours** come from the runtime's IANA data through `Intl`: `instantOf`, `localDateOf`,
   `openIntervals` and `addWorkingMinutes`. Local wall times survive clock changes.
6. **Recurrence** is RFC 5545 RRULE with FREQ (daily to yearly), INTERVAL, COUNT, UNTIL, BYDAY (with month positions),
   BYMONTHDAY, BYMONTH and WKST, expanded in a time zone through a required window. Other parts are refused, not
   ignored. Holidays of a given calendar are skipped; excepted and skipped dates still count toward COUNT.
7. **Slots** are start times where every segment that holds the resource lies in open time and overlaps no busy
   block; processing-gap segments may overlap anything.
8. **Age on a date** is whole years; the 29 February rule is a parameter from the pack (`LeapBirthday`).
9. **Not here.** Legal counting rules (trigger day, roll-forward, extensions) stay in the product's rules or deadline
   engine; display formatting stays in `@stackra/i18n`; calendar sync stays with connectors; the market-pack loader
   stays an open question of [0363](/adr/0363-configuration-over-code-business-behaviour-is-data-rules-workflows-and-studios).

## Consequences

- A new market's weekend or holidays is a pack edit; a late Hijri announcement is one dated rule.
- Deadline and delivery promises can warn on uncertainty instead of silently moving a date.
- Hermes and other engines must ship full ICU for Hijri and time zones; a missing calendar fails loudly.
- Callers merge pack and tenant rules themselves until the pack loader exists.
- RRULE parts outside the list need an amendment of this ADR before use.

## Enforcement

planned — the package's tests (`packages/foundation/calendar/src/**/__tests__/`) fix the behaviour and use made-up
fixtures only; the lint rule of [0363](/adr/0363-configuration-over-code-business-behaviour-is-data-rules-workflows-and-studios) will flag country or holiday literals in the source. Nothing checks it in CI
yet.
