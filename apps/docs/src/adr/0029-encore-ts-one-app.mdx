---
title: "0029 — Encore.ts as one application under services/"
description: "Proposed · 2026-10-19"
---

## Context

[0011](/adr/0011-rejected-and-deferred-technologies) defers Encore as the service runtime until the first service needs managed
databases, pub/sub or secrets. The owner has decided the backend runs on Encore.ts, and that trigger is met. [0010](/adr/0010-services-are-independent-workspace-members)
decision 6 already says that adopting Encore means one Encore application for the whole repository, but its layout (one
workspace member per service at `services/<name>`, each with its own entry) does not fit Encore, where an app is one
`encore.app` with a folder per service.

## Decision

1. **Encore.ts is the backend runtime.** The Encore row of [0011](/adr/0011-rejected-and-deferred-technologies) no longer applies.
2. **There is one Encore application**: a single `encore.app` under `services/`. Its gateway is the only gateway.
3. **One folder per Encore service** inside `services/`, declared by an `encore.service.ts`. A service never imports another service's source; calls use the generated client or events ([0015](/adr/0015-contract-packages)).
4. **Packages never import `encore.dev`, and apps never import a service** ([0003](/adr/0003-tier-dependency-direction)).
5. **Workspace shape:** how the single `encore.app` maps to workspace members, `tier:service` tags and `@stackra/service-<name>` names is TODO and decided in the first-service spec. Until then 0010 decisions 1 and 5 are amended only as far as stated here.
6. The Encore plus legacy-decorator combination ([0005](/adr/0005-di-container-and-legacy-decorators)) is proven by a spike before any service depends on it.

## Consequences

- Managed Postgres, Pub/Sub, cron, secrets and tracing come from one platform.
- Services deploy as one unit; independent per-service deploys are given up.
- The `AGENTS.md` "Not built yet" row for Encore and the `services/example`, `services/health` layout need revising when this is accepted.

## Enforcement

`boundaries.jsonc` keeps `encore.dev` out of packages. Planned — `validate-repository.mjs` check that exactly one `encore.app` exists and sits under `services/`.

## Supersedes and sources

Will supersede [0011](/adr/0011-rejected-and-deferred-technologies) in part (the Encore deferral row). Amends [0010](/adr/0010-services-are-independent-workspace-members) (layout). Sources: owner decisions in chat, 2026-10-19.
