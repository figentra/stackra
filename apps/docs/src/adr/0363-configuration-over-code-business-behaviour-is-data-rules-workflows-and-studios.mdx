## Context

The owner ruled on 2026-10-25 that every business answer is a default configuration, seeded as data and editable in
each product's admin. Several Stackra ADRs still carry business values: the plan ladder in [0242](/adr/0242-commercial-model-plan-ladder-add-ons-metric-and-trial), tier names in [0203](/adr/0203-the-commercial-model-billing-provider-and-tier-names),
country tax rules "in OS code" in [0220](/adr/0220-stackra-money-and-the-ledger-package) and [0268](/adr/0268-register-answers-thin-items-r1-to-r18), named privacy laws in [0189](/adr/0189-privacy-operations-and-data-retention) and [0343](/adr/0343-cross-tenant-record-sharing-consented-copies-fed-by-events), and a 100-recipient threshold in
[0360](/adr/0360-the-ai-chain-four-layers-one-spine-approval-modes-and-the-never-bypass-floor). A mechanism that names a country or a price cannot serve a second market or a second product without a code
change. Stackra already has the pieces: the settings cascade ([0213](/adr/0213-settings-v2-cascade-flags-as-a-kind-yaml-packs-and-revisions)), the rules engine ([0331](/adr/0331-stackra-rules-one-condition-engine-for-every-rule-consumer)), the workflow engine
([0153](/adr/0153-automations-run-as-principals-and-every-package-exposes-building-blocks)), the builder document kinds ([0270](/adr/0270-builder-architecture-one-editor-kernel-document-kinds-and-collaboration-levels)) and provider choice per Market ([0356](/adr/0356-choosing-a-provider-per-tenant-and-market-through-the-connection-with-a-market-default)).

## Decision

1. **Code holds mechanism only.** Engines, ports, drivers, studios and importers are code. Business behaviour is not:
   prices, plans, tiers, seat kinds, modules, fees, limits, trials, offers, policies and thresholds are data.
2. **Four homes for business behaviour.**
   - **Data:** catalogue records and settings, edited in each product's admin.
   - **Rules:** any decision with a condition is a rule set in `@stackra/rules` ([0331](/adr/0331-stackra-rules-one-condition-engine-for-every-rule-consumer)). Return windows, deposits, cash
     on delivery, approvals, tips, service charges, discount permits and commissions are rule sets.
   - **Workflows:** any multi-step flow is a workflow template ([0153](/adr/0153-automations-run-as-principals-and-every-package-exposes-building-blocks)).
   - **Studios:** screens, forms, dashboards, reports and print documents are builder documents ([0270](/adr/0270-builder-architecture-one-editor-kernel-document-kinds-and-collaboration-levels)).
3. **Market packs.** A country or legal area is a market pack: versioned data with effective dates that holds tax,
   e-invoicing, till fiscal rules, legal floors, carriers, payment and messaging providers, sign-in and verification
   methods, holidays, address format, currency and languages. Overlays (a free zone, an EU member state) extend a base
   pack. No Stackra package names a country. A driver may serve one country; choosing it is pack data through the
   Connection rule of [0356](/adr/0356-choosing-a-provider-per-tenant-and-market-through-the-connection-with-a-market-default).
4. **Legal floors are pack data.** A legal minimum or maximum (a withdrawal period, a reporting window, a retention
   period, a tax rate) is a floor in the pack. The settings resolver and the rules engine refuse a value past the floor
   and say which pack and law set it. Code holds the check, never the number.
5. **Settings layers.** The cascade of [0213](/adr/0213-settings-v2-cascade-flags-as-a-kind-yaml-packs-and-revisions) decision 2 is read as four business layers: platform floor, product
   default, tenant, user. A narrower layer may only tighten a floor. Each key declares live or sandbox ([0359](/adr/0359-modes-and-configuration-environments-display-modes-sandbox-promotion-and-live-records) decisions
   3 to 5).
6. **Plan catalogue.** Each product's tiers, seat kinds, modules, limits, trials and offers are records in a plan
   catalogue read by entitlements ([0215](/adr/0215-entitlements)) and billing ([0358](/adr/0358-the-stackra-licence-and-one-billing-engine-with-the-seller-as-a-parameter)). A seat meter counts the principal kinds the catalogue
   names as seats. An ADR may name the kinds of limit; it never fixes their values.
7. **Seeds.** A product's defaults ship as its seed pack ([0325](/adr/0325-launch-readiness-release-rules-edge-caching-and-update-strategy) decision 7, [0190](/adr/0190-seed-profiles-and-product-analytics)), loaded into the platform and into
   every new sandbox. Changing a default is a seed change and a data migration, not a release of code.
8. **Thresholds in floors.** A platform safety threshold (for example the bulk-send size of [0360](/adr/0360-the-ai-chain-four-layers-one-spine-approval-modes-and-the-never-bypass-floor) decision 8) is a
   platform-floor setting. A tenant may make it stricter, never looser.
9. **Tests check shape, not values.** A test may check that the seed matches its schema and respects the floors. It
   never asserts a business number copied from an ADR.

## Consequences

- A new country is a new pack and its drivers, not a code change. A new price or plan is an admin edit.
- `@stackra/settings` needs a floor-aware resolver, and `@stackra/rules` needs to read pack floors.
- A market-pack loader, a plan-catalogue model and a seed compiler need homes (see `stackra-package-needs.md`).
- ADRs get shorter: they keep the mechanism and point to the seed for values.
- Reviewers must refuse a pull request that adds a country branch, a price or a policy value to code.

## Enforcement

planned — a lint rule that flags country codes, currency codes and tier names in package source outside packs and
tests; a seed-schema test per product; a resolver test that a tenant value past a pack floor is refused with the
pack and law named.

## Open questions

1. **Pack home.** Whether the market-pack loader is a new package or part of `@stackra/settings` is not decided.
2. **Pack authorship.** Who signs a pack version (counsel, a local reviewer) before it goes live is not decided.
3. **Floor conflicts across packs.** For a tenant active in two markets, which pack wins for a shared record (the
   strictest, or the pack of the record's legal entity) is not decided.
