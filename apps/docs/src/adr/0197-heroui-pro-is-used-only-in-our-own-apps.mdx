## Context

[0113](/adr/0113-heroui-pro-distribution) (Accepted) let HeroUI Pro reach customers through a mirror bundle and a bring-your-own-key option. The owner's answer OR9 (mem L579, 2026-10-20) suspends that until the OEM request (STA-41) is answered.

## Decision

1. **Scope.** HeroUI Pro is used only in Stackra's own apps, until an OEM agreement exists (OR9, STA-41).
2. **Packages.** Free packages never depend on Pro components (mem L579).
3. **Suspended.** The mirror bundle and the bring-your-own-key option are suspended.
4. **Access.** Our apps and CI keep `HEROUI_PERSONAL_TOKEN`.

## Consequences

- A customer's app built on Stackra gets the free HeroUI components only.
- Lifting the restriction is a new ADR, after STA-41.

## Enforcement

planned — the validator rejects a free package that depends on a Pro component.

## Open questions

1. STA-41, the HeroUI OEM answer. This ADR waits for it (register thin item 20).
