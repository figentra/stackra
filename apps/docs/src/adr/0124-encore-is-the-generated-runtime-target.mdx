## Context

[0011](/adr/0011-rejected-and-deferred-technologies) lists Encore as the service runtime with status Deferred, and
[0010](/adr/0010-services-are-independent-workspace-members) decisions 5 and 6 say the runtime is not decided and describe
what would follow "if Encore is adopted". Since then the owner locked the generated-app design (Round 9 G3) and
[0091](/adr/0091-generated-encore-app-in-stackra-folder) was accepted: `stackra gen` emits a complete Encore application into a
gitignored `.stackra/encore/`, and services hold no Encore files. The rules, docs pages and the session hook still repeat the
"deferred" status, so agents are told two opposite things. This ADR records what Accepted 0091 already decided, in the two
places that contradict it; it decides nothing new.

## Decision

1. **Encore.ts is the backend runtime, as a generated target only.** The Encore application exists only as the output of
   `stackra gen` in `.stackra/encore/` ([0091](/adr/0091-generated-encore-app-in-stackra-folder) decisions 2, 5 and 6).
2. **Service members stay plain TypeScript.** Nothing under `services/` contains an `encore.*` file, a `__generated__/`
   folder or an import of `encore.dev` or `~encore/*` (0091 decision 1).
3. **Packages never import `encore.dev`**, as today (`packages/tooling/oxlint/src/boundaries.jsonc`).
4. **Only `app-manifest.json` is committed** from the generated app, and `stackra gen --check` fails CI when it is stale
   (0091 decisions 3 and 4).
5. **The Encore row of 0011 is superseded.** It no longer reads "Deferred"; the not-built-yet rule lists hand-written Encore in
   service source or packages as rejected, with `stackra gen` as the way to use Encore.
6. **0010 decisions 5 and 6 are amended.** The runtime is decided (decision 1 here). Decision 6's "one Encore application for
   the repository" holds for the generated app; its gateway and `auth` defaults follow
   [0093](/adr/0093-better-auth-integration) and the generator, not hand-written endpoints.

Still open, and not decided here (gap questionnaire IDs):

- **DP2:** how the gitignored generated app reaches Encore Cloud or another runtime; no deploy path exists yet.
- **FW3:** how plain-TypeScript services declare secrets, databases and buckets for gen to emit.
- **DP12 and FW4:** how MikroORM migrations, including migrations shipped in packages, reach Encore's runner.
- **FW1 and B53:** the service member's shape (`src/main.ts` of 0010 decision 1 versus a root module in `package.json`, 0090).

## Consequences

- Rules, docs and hooks stop calling Encore deferred; they point at 0091 and this ADR.
- An agent asked to add an Encore endpoint, topic or cron writes decorated TypeScript in the service and lets gen emit the
  Encore code; it never writes `encore.service.ts` or imports `encore.dev` in a member.
- The deploy runbook stays a placeholder until DP2 is answered.

## Enforcement

Existing: `packages/tooling/oxlint/src/boundaries.jsonc` bans `encore.dev` in packages. Planned (0091): `stackra gen --check` in
CI and `pnpm verify`; a validator check that no `encore.*` file or `__generated__/` folder exists under `services/` and that
`.stackra/` is gitignored. Until the scaffold's `services/encore` template is replaced (gap B4), `checkServiceUnit` in
`tools/scripts/validate-repository.mjs` still describes the older layout.
