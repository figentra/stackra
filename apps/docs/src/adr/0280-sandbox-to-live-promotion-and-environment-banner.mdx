## Context

A tenant builds its configuration in a sandbox and then needs it in live without re-entering it. People also act in the wrong environment
when nothing on screen says which one they are in. The owner's answer to the screenshot gap review of 2026-10-21 (item 11) adds promotion and a
banner.

## Decision

1. **Promotion copies configuration.** Configuration is copied or promoted from a sandbox to live: the catalog, settings packs and webhooks.
2. **Keys are never copied.** API keys and other credentials are never copied; live gets newly issued keys.
3. **Where it runs.** Promotion runs through the sandboxes module of `@figentra/service-tenancy` and the packs of `@figentra/service-settings`.
4. **Reviewed diff.** Promotion is shown as a diff between sandbox and live, and applies only after review.
5. **Environment banner.** A persistent environment banner (sandbox, staging) is shown in the `@stackra/panel` PanelShell and in apps.

## Consequences

- Live configuration has a reviewable origin, and a credential never crosses environments.
- Each promotable kind needs a diff and an apply step, which is work for the owners of the catalog, settings and webhooks.
- The banner costs a strip of screen on non-production environments.

## Enforcement

planned — a promotion test asserts no key or secret appears in the copied set; a panel test asserts the banner renders on every non-production
environment and not in production.

## Open details

The owner did not give these; the conservative default below applies until an ADR changes it.

1. **Who approves.** An owner or administrator of the live tenant; applying is a high-risk action that needs the step-up of
   [0207](/adr/0207-authentication-assurance-and-machine-identities) decision 1.
2. **Webhooks.** Endpoints and subscriptions are copied; signing secrets are new, like keys.
3. **Data.** Tenant business data (customers, orders, records) is never promoted.
4. **Banner.** Cannot be dismissed; production shows none.
5. **Direction.** Sandbox to live only; live to sandbox is a reset, not a promotion.
