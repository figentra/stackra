## Context

Every product release ships seven languages: Arabic, English, French, German, Spanish, Italian and Dutch (RV-55a makes
this a release blocker). `@stackra/i18n` knew four base languages and four overlays, formatted times with whatever clock
the engine guessed, and had no week start. The framework UI packages shipped English and Arabic or nothing, with
English strings left in component code. Nothing failed when a translation missed a key. The US and Dutch markets need
imperial display over metric storage and their own postal formats.

## Decision

1. **Locales.** `Locale` holds the seven base languages `ar`, `en`, `fr`, `de`, `es`, `it`, `nl` and the overlays
   `ar-SA`, `ar-AE`, `ar-EG`, `en-US` and `es-US`. Which locales a Market offers, and its binding legal language, stay
   `market-pack:<code>.languages[]` data.
2. **Formats as case data with an override.** Each `Locale` case carries its CLDR default first day of the week (ISO:
   Monday for the European languages, `ar-AE` and `en`; Sunday for `en-US`, `es-US` and `ar-SA`; Saturday for `ar` and
   `ar-EG`) and its clock (`HourCycle`: 24-hour for fr, de, es, it and nl; 12-hour otherwise). `I18nConfig.formats`
   overrides either per locale, and `localeFormat(locale, override)` merges them. The provider puts the result in
   context and builds every Intl tag with `-u-hc-<cycle>-nu-<digits>`. Decimal and grouping separators come from Intl
   (comma decimal in fr, de, es, it and nl); no separator table is written.
3. **Imperial display over metric storage.** Storage stays metric `Quantity`. `DisplayUnit` (grams to gallons) and
   `formatQuantity(quantity, unit, options)` convert exactly and format with Intl's unit names; the unit a Market or a
   user sees is pack data or a user setting. `formatLength` stays for lengths.
4. **Postal formats are pack data.** The Italian CAP (`^\d{5}$`) and the Dutch postcode
   (`^([1-9]\d{3})(?!SA|SD|SS)([A-Z]{2})$`, stored `1234 AB`) are `identity.address_format` values in the `it` and
   `nl` packs. `IdentifierFormat` gains `canonical`, a `$n` template that writes the stored form from the pattern's
   groups (a group that did not match drops with the text before it). No country is named in code.
5. **Analysers.** Spanish, Italian and Dutch use the Postgres built-in `spanish`, `italian` and `dutch` configurations,
   already in `TEXT_SEARCH_CONFIGS`; a `search_vector_<locale>` column picks its configuration from its language
   subtag, so `es-US` uses `spanish`.
6. **Reindex plan for the new languages.** A model that adds a language adds its `search_vector_<locale>` column with
   `t.searchable({ locales })` in a new migration (expand), builds its GIN index concurrently in the next migration,
   then runs `search.in(Model).reindex()` as a job in its own context. Until the reindex finishes, rows without the new
   vector are found through the other lanes; nothing is dropped.
7. **Framework catalogues.** `ui`, `ui-pro` (`ui_pro`), `panel`, `consent` and `notifications` ship
   `assets/lang/<language>/<namespace>.json` in all seven languages. The `ui` keys are the ones its components name in
   their shortcut comments; moving each component from its English constant to `useTranslation` follows in the `ui`
   package's own change. Every non-English catalogue is reviewed by a native speaker before it ships (0192).
8. **Completeness gate.** `tools/scripts/catalogue-completeness.mjs`, run inside `pnpm validate`, fails when a base
   language misses a namespace or a key of `en`, when any catalogue holds a key `en` lacks, when a member has no `en`,
   and when one of the five framework members lacks one of the seven languages. Overlays may hold fewer keys.
9. **Amount in words.** Stays a pack option for `ar` and `en` only; no speller is added for the new languages. Voice
   languages stay pack data.

## Consequences

- A member may list `es`, `it` and `nl` in `I18nConfig.locales`; the fallback rules are unchanged.
- A 12-hour or Sunday-first market on a European language is a configuration change, not code.
- A translator who drops a key fails `pnpm validate` with the file and the key.
- Every translated message names the same ICU placeholders as its `en` source: the argument names, including the ones
  nested in `plural`, `select` and `selectordinal` options, compared as a set. Quoted text and the plural `#` are not
  arguments. A translator who drops, adds or renames a `{name}`, or writes a message that does not parse as ICU, fails
  `pnpm validate` with the file and the key.

## Enforcement

automated — `tools/scripts/catalogue-completeness.mjs` in `pnpm validate`; the package tests fix the locales, formats
and postal formats (`packages/foundation/i18n/src/core/**/__tests__/`, `packages/foundation/validation/src/core/functions/__tests__/`).
