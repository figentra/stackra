## Context

The wave 10 hand-over (`notes/handover/build-wave10-2026-10-25.md`) ends with owner questions per builder. Several were
answered by later rounds without being fed back (R3-SO-19); others need an ADR, which 0371 to 0383 now propose. The
build waves that depend on them need one place that says, per question, what the answer is or where it is decided.
Numbers below are the builder's own question numbers.
