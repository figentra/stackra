## Context

[0017](/adr/0017-agent-roster-and-models) (Accepted) named an agent roster with economy, standard and frontier tiers. The owner's answers B5, B6, B7 and the roster decisions (mem L389–394, 2026-10-20) replace the tiers and fix who merges.

## Decision

1. **Tiers (B6).** Five tiers replace economy, standard and frontier: steward, executor, reviewer, guardian and lead.
2. **Models.** Opus leads. Fable is used only for ADR and constitution reversals and deep audits. Guardians run on Opus, reviewers and executors on Sonnet, and stewards on Haiku.
3. **Roster.**
   - The package steward is folded into the code-standards steward.
   - A code reviewer is added, on Sonnet, escalating to Opus.
   - OpenCode gets only the steward and executor rows, on Kimi K3 (B5).
   - The user-level drizzle, nestjs and expo-router skills move to the Academorix and Figentra repositories.
4. **Merging (B7).** Claude commits, pushes and merges through `pr:merge`, under the merge rule of the register (after a green gate and an independent reviewer PASS). Releases and production promotion stay with a human.
5. **Routing.** `.agents/routing.yaml` is updated, and the stale `sdlc:*` routes are fixed (gap B11).

## Consequences

- A tier names what the agent does, not what it costs.
- Fable's cost is limited to reversals and audits, so routine work does not use it.

## Enforcement

planned — `pnpm validate` checks `models.yaml` and `routing.yaml` against the roster; `pnpm agents:check` checks the projections.

## Open questions

1. None for the roster. The OpenCode row depends on the Kimi K3 availability.

## Alternatives

- Economy, standard and frontier tiers: superseded by B6.
