## Context

Outcome kinds were runtime values (`Rules.create({ outcomes })`), so gen could not list what a rule may yield. Floors
guarded numbers only, though fee caps and deposit limits are money. A tenant who set an outcome past a floor learned it
only when the guard moved the value at decision time. Access stores condition trees from untrusted input and had only
its own shape check, with no way to check another service's fields at write time.

## Decision

1. **Declared outcome kinds.** `class RefundWindow extends RuleOutcome({ key, label, params }) {}`, one per
   `<name>.outcome.ts` in the module's `rules/` group. `Rules.create` takes the class or the data form. Gen reads the
   options from source and lists each kind in the manifest's `outcomes` section as `<module>.<key>` with its label and
   parameters, locked by class.
2. **Money floors.** A money parameter may name a floor; the pack value is `{ minor, currency }`. A money amount in
   another currency counts as crossing the floor and is moved to the floor's own amount (the engine never converts).
   One comparison, `crossedFloor`, serves the guard and the save.
3. **Save-time refusal.** Given the market's floors, `Rules.checkOutcomes`, `RuleSetService.draft` and the rule builder
   refuse an outcome past a floor (`past_floor`, `currency_mismatch` at `<index>.params.<name>`) instead of moving it.
   `guardFloors` stays the final guard at decision time. `RuleBuilder` edits outcomes: a registered kind and a field per
   parameter.
4. **Untrusted conditions.** `Condition$` checks a tree's shape (200 nodes, 8 levels, known operators, field paths);
   `rules.conditionSchema()` checks it against a registry, each problem a zod issue at its node. A service publishes
   its fields as `FactSource$` documents so another service builds that registry and checks fields at write time.

## Consequences

- The studio, docs and AI tools read outcome kinds from the manifest.
- Packs carry money floors with their currency; a pack whose floor value does not fit its parameter throws.

## Enforcement

`packages/application/rules/src/core/{functions,values,constants,bases}/__tests__/` and
`packages/developer/gen/src/core/functions/__tests__/build-manifest.function.test.ts` (the `outcomes` section).
