## Context

Customers connect Stackra to other systems, and each connection holds credentials. The owner's integration answers IN1 to IN4 (mem L753–756, 2026-10-20) fix the connector SDK, the connections per level, and the sync rules.

## Decision

1. **Connector SDK (IN1).** `@stackra/integrations` is the connector SDK for first-party connectors. An adapter runs the MIT-licensed Activepieces pieces, with a licence check per piece in CI.
2. **Connections per level (IN2).** A connection can sit at the platform, product, tenant, scope or user level. It is stored in the store of ADR 0107, and referenced with `@ConnectionRef`. Refresh, expiry alerts, health checks and revocation apply to every connection.
3. **Triggers and syncs (IN3).** Triggers, actions and managed two-way syncs.
4. **Hosting.** The connections store is hosted by the integrations service (gap C12).
5. **Marketplace (IN4).** The marketplace model is an OS item.

## Consequences

- A credential is held at the level that owns it, so a tenant's connection is never visible to its parent by default.
- A piece with a licence that does not match fails the CI check before it ships.

## Enforcement

planned — the per-piece licence check runs in CI; the connection health check runs on schedule.
