## Context

[0175](/adr/0175-edge-domains-and-tls) puts tenant custom domains on Cloudflare for SaaS and defers the flow to a tenant domain ADR, which did not
exist. The owner's answer to the screenshot gap review of 2026-10-21 (item 10) settles the flow in the `domains` module of
`@figentra/service-tenancy`.

## Decision

1. **Hostname registry.** service-tenancy keeps a registry of tenant hostnames. A hostname belongs to at most one tenant. The registry
   refuses our own zones and any hostname already verified by another tenant.
2. **Ownership first.** The tenant proves ownership with a TXT record, and adds the ACME TXT record for the certificate. Only then is a CNAME to
   the edge accepted. The ownership TXT value is a random token generated for each tenant, hostname and attempt, so a value cannot be reused
   across tenants or replayed after a failed attempt.
3. **Verification states.** Each hostname moves through named verification states (open detail 1), and the console shows the current state and
   the record still missing.
4. **TLS.** Certificates are issued and renewed through Cloudflare for SaaS (0175 decision 2), configured by `@stackra/infra-cloudflare`.
5. **Event.** Successful verification emits `tenancy.domain.verified.v1`.
6. **Primary domain.** A tenant has one primary domain. Its other verified domains redirect to it.
7. **Removal and re-verification.** A tenant can remove a domain, which releases it from the registry. A verified domain is re-verified
   periodically, and a domain that fails re-verification stops serving (open detail 2).
8. **No dangling hostname.** When a domain is removed, fails or expires, its Cloudflare custom hostname is deleted at once.
9. **Domain claim.** The domain claim of
   [0243](/adr/0243-organisations-on-the-tenant-tree-consolidated-billing-guardrails-and-standalone-exit) decision 7 (auto-join, inviting
   personal workspaces on the domain) reuses this registry and its ownership proof.

## Consequences

- A tenant cannot point a hostname at the edge before proving it owns the zone, so one tenant cannot take another's hostname.
- Certificates are the edge provider's chore, not ours; on-prem keeps its own certificates (0175 decision 2).
- Other services react to `tenancy.domain.verified.v1` instead of polling the registry.

## Enforcement

planned — a state-machine test covers each transition and asserts the Cloudflare custom hostname is deleted on removal, failure and expiry;
a registry test refuses our own zones and a hostname verified by another tenant; a test asserts a TXT token from another tenant or an earlier
attempt does not verify; the event name is checked by the event-name rule of [0141](/adr/0141-integration-event-names-and-envelope).

## Open details

The owner did not give these; the conservative default below applies until an ADR changes it.

1. **States.** `pending_ownership`, `pending_dns`, `pending_certificate`, `active`, `failed` and `removed` (snake_case machine codes, per
   [0231](/adr/0231-naming-and-casing-standard)).
2. **Re-verification.** Daily. After 7 days of failure the domain moves to `failed`, stops serving, and the owner is notified; the primary then
   falls back to the tenant's handle URL.
3. **Pending expiry.** A hostname that stays unverified for 14 days is released from the registry.
4. **Limits.** The number of domains per tenant comes from the plan entitlements.
