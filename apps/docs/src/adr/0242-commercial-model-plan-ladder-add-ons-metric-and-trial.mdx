## Context

Figentra sells several products (Academorix, Juristra, Retail OS and others) on one account. The owner's commercial answers of 2026-10-20 night (mem L947–952, the last entries of the decision file) fix the buying unit, the metric, the ladder, and the trial. The first ladder had a Free column, and the owner's final answer dropped it.

## Decision

1. **Buying unit.** A plan ladder for each product, plus shared platform add-ons on the one account (AI credits, extra storage, SSO, advanced audit, marketplace apps). Add-ons work across products.
2. **Metric.** Hybrid: a platform fee per tier, plus seats for internal users only (a tenant's end customers are never seats), plus metered usage for the cost drivers: AI credits, storage in GB, SMS, WhatsApp and email messages, automation runs, and API calls over the included amount.
3. **Tiers.** Starter, Pro, Business and Enterprise. There is no Free plan (memory L952, final).
4. **Ladder (placeholders, editable per product in the console).** Seats, scope depth, storage, AI credits, automation runs, API rate, and the other limits are in the table below. The owner's ladder was given for five columns; the four-column values are the accepted ladder (see open questions).

| Limit                        | Starter   | Pro       | Business      | Enterprise             |
| ---------------------------- | --------- | --------- | ------------- | ---------------------- |
| Seats (internal users)       | 10        | 50        | 250           | custom                 |
| Scope depth below the tenant | 2         | 4         | 8             | unlimited              |
| Storage                      | 25 GB     | 250 GB    | 1 TB          | custom                 |
| AI credits per month         | 2k        | 20k       | 100k          | custom                 |
| Automation runs per month    | 5k        | 50k       | 500k          | custom                 |
| API rate                     | 300 rpm   | 1.2k rpm  | 6k rpm        | custom                 |
| Audit retention              | 30 days   | 90 days   | 13 months     | custom, with export    |
| Sandbox environments         | 0         | 1         | 3             | custom                 |
| Custom domain                | yes       | yes       | yes           | yes                    |
| White-label                  | no        | yes       | yes           | yes                    |
| SSO                          | —         | add-on    | included      | included               |
| SCIM                         | no        | no        | yes           | yes                    |
| Region choice                | no        | no        | yes           | dedicated              |
| Support                      | email     | priority  | 24-5 with SLA | 24-7 with TAM          |
| Marketplace apps             | free apps | free apps | free apps     | private apps           |
| On-prem and air-gap          | no        | no        | no            | yes, by Keygen licence |

5. **Trial.** A new tenant gets a 14-day Pro trial with no card. It needs email and phone verification, and one trial per organisation.
6. **After the trial.** The tenant chooses a paid plan. If it does not, the tenant goes read-only. Its data is kept for 30 days and stays exportable, then it is deleted with notice.
7. **Every gate is an entitlement.** Code is free, and the runtime gate reads the plan (ADR 0215). A tenant calling a feature it is not entitled to gets "requires subscription" with the upgrade path.

## Consequences

- A price page can only list what the table lists, because the tiers and limits are data in the entitlement system.
- Read-only with a 30-day export is the end of the trial, so a tenant is never deleted without a warning and a way out.

## Enforcement

planned — the entitlement table is checked against this ADR by a test; the trial state machine follows the tenant lifecycle (ADR 0210).

## Open questions

1. **Settled.** The four-column mapping is the accepted ladder as written: the Starter, Pro, Business and Enterprise columns. Free is dropped (memory L952).
2. **Settled.** Scope depth is Starter 2, Pro 4, Business 8 and Enterprise unlimited, as the ladder row above says (owner answer, "Part-D clarifications"). It replaces the earlier "Business and above unlimited" line (memory L878).
3. **Settled.** Starter support is email, as the ladder column says.
