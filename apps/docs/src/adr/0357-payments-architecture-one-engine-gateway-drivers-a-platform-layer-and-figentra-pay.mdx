## Context

Commerce, Muslin, Academorix fees, Juristra invoices and Figentra's own billing all take payments. Academorix (Stripe
Connect, dunning, disputes) and Muslin (split tender, gift cards, Paymob, Tabby) each carry their own payment code, and
no gateway port exists in Stackra. [0219](/adr/0219-the-merchant-account-chain-and-registered-price-models) fixes the
merchant chain, the payment modes and the fee cascade, and [0220](/adr/0220-stackra-money-and-the-ledger-package) the
money and ledger primitives, but not where payments run or which providers back them. The owner decided the
architecture on 2026-10-25 (Commerce questionnaire Answers and 27 to 30; OS rounds 5, 13 and 14), and replaced the
earlier "port from the products" default with one engine built once.

## Decision

**One engine.**

1. Payments run once, in Figentra OS: the payments service and the payments-ledger service. `@stackra/payments` holds
   the ports. Every product uses that engine; no product keeps its own payment code, and Academorix and Muslin code is
   not mixed in.
2. The engine has three layers:
   - **gateway drivers**: one driver per provider behind the gateway port;
   - **a Connect-style platform layer**: merchant onboarding with KYC, splits, the platform fee and payouts, through a
     licensed provider's platform product;
   - **Figentra Pay**: branded checkout, saved methods, payment links, refunds and wallets.

**Gateways and providers.**

3. **Gateway set per market** (Commerce 27): KSA Moyasar or HyperPay, plus Tabby and Tamara; UAE Checkout.com or
   Stripe; Egypt Paymob and Fawry, plus Valu; EU Stripe. The gateway for a payment is chosen per tenant and Market
   through the Connection ([0356](/adr/0356-choosing-a-provider-per-tenant-and-market-through-the-connection-with-a-market-default)).
4. **Platform layer providers** (OS-91): Checkout.com for KSA and UAE, Paymob for Egypt, Stripe Connect for the EU and
   US.

**Figentra Pay.**

5. Figentra Pay is shown in each product's brand: Academorix Pay, Muslin Pay, Juristra Pay. One engine serves every
   brand.
6. **Merchant accounts.** The merchant chooses:
   - its **own gateway account**: no Figentra transaction fee at launch (OS-93); or
   - **Figentra's platform account**: one blended rate per Market (provider cost plus a markup, lower markup on higher
     tiers) (OS-92). When a payment is refunded, the platform fee is refunded pro rata (OS-101).
7. **Closed-loop wallet only.** Store credit, gift cards, loyalty and a prepaid balance at the same merchant. Top-up is
   limited to buying gift cards or a per-store prepaid balance, capped per Market. No transfer between people and
   no cash-out (OS-97). An open wallet comes later, through a licensed e-money partner or a licence. Gift card and
   wallet balances live in payments-ledger; loyalty is a shared kit (OS-343).

**Safety rules.**

8. **PCI SAQ-A** (Commerce 28). Hosted fields or redirect only; Figentra never touches card numbers. Terminals are
   semi-integrated. A saved card is a gateway token.
9. **The `unknown` state** (Commerce 29). A payment whose result is lost (for example a terminal that drops) enters an
   `unknown` state. It is resolved by a lookup at the provider before any retry. A manager may resolve it by hand,
   with a reason.
10. **KYC and KYB** (OS-54). The OS verification service checks a business once (CR, tax number, documents, bank
    letter, IBAN, owners and beneficial owners, IDs, selfie liveness, sanctions and PEP). Every product and every Pay
    brand reuses the result, and it is passed to the payment provider's own KYC. It replaces Academorix's own KYC/KYB
    at its rewrite.
11. **Legal role** (OS-421, OS-94). Figentra's regulatory role is decided after launch. Until then the design rule
    holds: only the licensed provider holds money; Figentra never does. Counsel confirms per market before the first
    live payment.

**Settled by their recommended default (owner rule, `default-applied.md`).**

12. Payouts follow the provider's default schedule per market; a rolling reserve only when a risk rule fires (OS-95).
    A closed-loop balance is spendable anywhere in the tenant in its issue currency (OS-96). Gift card expiry is a
    tenant rule with each Market's legal minimum as a floor (OS-98). Loyalty points redeem as a discount (OS-99). A
    refund goes to the original tender by default (OS-100).
13. In a marketplace split, the seller of the line carries refunds and chargebacks (OS-102). A dispute centre ships at
    launch for platform accounts, with a read-only mirror for own accounts (OS-103). Gateway settlement reports and
    terminal batches are matched to the ledger automatically; bank feeds later (OS-104). Bank transfers are recorded by
    hand with proof at launch (OS-105). Deposits and partial capture ship at launch; tips and instalment plans later
    (OS-106). The provider's settlement FX rate is snapshotted (OS-107).
14. Sandbox gateways: see [0359](/adr/0359-modes-and-configuration-environments-display-modes-sandbox-promotion-and-live-records) decision 11.

**Cash.**

15. **Cash rounding** (Commerce answer 34). A `@stackra/money` table per currency and market sets the rounding step. It
    applies to cash only, and the difference is posted on its own ledger line.

## Consequences

- One payment engine to build, certify and secure, instead of one per product.
- Every product gets platform payments, wallets and payouts under its own brand without writing payment code.
- Licensed providers hold funds and run their own checks, so the platform account can open without Figentra holding
  money; the legal sign-off per market is still on the critical path.
- KSA and UAE platform payments go through Checkout.com while a KSA merchant's own account may be Moyasar or HyperPay,
  so the gateway port must serve both paths.
- Academorix and Muslin move onto the engine at their rewrite.

## Enforcement

planned — the gateway, platform-layer, dispute and settlement ports in `@stackra/payments` with a conformance suite
per driver (including the `unknown` state and lookup-before-retry); a log and access-log redaction test that no card
number is ever accepted or logged; the validator check of round-3 #38 that no vendor SDK is reached outside a driver;
the wallet actions refusing transfer and cash-out.

## Open questions

1. **Who issues which invoice on the platform account (OS-94).** The owner grouped OS-94 with the legal-role question
   decided after launch, so whether the merchant issues the sale invoice and Figentra a monthly fee invoice is open.
2. **"Or" in the gateway set.** KSA "Moyasar or HyperPay" and UAE "Checkout.com or Stripe": whether both drivers are
   built for launch or one is chosen per market is not decided.
3. **Commerce's own Pay brand.** The brands named are Academorix Pay, Muslin Pay and Juristra Pay. The name for
   Figentra Commerce itself (and for OS sold alone) is not given.
4. **Prepaid top-up caps** per Market are not set. Whether "store" in decision 7 means a store brand or the whole
   merchant is also open (OS-96 lets a balance be spent anywhere in the tenant, with an optional limit to one brand).
5. **Card terminal provider** for the semi-integrated terminals is not named.
6. **Which repository records this.** The Figentra OS repository holds uncommitted ADRs on the same payments
   decisions. Which repository keeps which decision is open; see
   [0358](/adr/0358-money-plans-and-pricing-four-levels-one-billing-engine-and-hybrid-pricing) open question 5.
