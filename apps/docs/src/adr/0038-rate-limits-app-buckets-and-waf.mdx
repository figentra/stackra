## Context

Abuse needs stopping at two points: at the edge before it costs compute, and in the app where the caller's identity and plan are known.

## Decision

1. **Cloudflare WAF and rate rules guard the edge** against volumetric and unauthenticated abuse.
2. **The application enforces token buckets** per tenant, per token and per endpoint class, keyed on authenticated identity.
3. A limited call returns `429` with `Retry-After`.
4. Bucket sizes and plan-based limits: TODO; they tie to tenant entitlements, which are decided separately.

## Consequences

- Two layers to configure; edge rules are outside the Encore app.

## Enforcement

Planned — a shared rate-limit helper with a test; Cloudflare rules kept as code, with a drift check.
