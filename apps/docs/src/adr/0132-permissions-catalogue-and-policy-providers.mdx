---
title: "0132 — Permissions are a module catalogue; policies are providers"
description: "Accepted · 2026-10-20"
---

## Context

Permissions were a flat list, and nothing checked that an action's permission made sense for what the action does. The
owner's intent-map item P2 and the IAM answer A10 (mem L627) set a curated catalogue per module, with labels and risk, and
policy providers that decide per request. P2 and A10 use a class for the catalogue, not a `define*` function.

## Decision

1. Each module declares its permissions in a `@Permissions` class.
2. Keys are `<module>.<resource>.<action>`. Each catalogue entry carries a label, a description, a risk and a verb (A10).
3. The catalogue is curated per module. Gen **warns**, and does not fail, when an action reuses a permission whose verb
   differs from the action's verb (mem L401).
4. A `@Policy` provider method returns `allow`, `deny` or `abstain`. Any `deny` wins.
5. Wildcard permissions are allowed only in system roles (A10).
6. A rename of a permission goes through deprecation ([0131](/adr/0131-module-key-version-and-deprecation)).
7. The `define*` family in the 2026-10-19 DI answer (B20) did not include `definePermissions`. The owner must confirm
   (register contradiction C-04). `defineToken` and `defineConfig` are unaffected
   (ADRs 0145 and 0146, wave 1 part 2).

## Consequences

- Permission review becomes a catalogue review, per module.
- A verb mismatch is a warning, so an existing action is not broken on the day the check lands.

## Enforcement

planned — gen emits the catalogue from `@Permissions` and warns on a verb mismatch. Wildcard use outside system roles is
planned as a gen error.

## Alternatives

- A `definePermissions()` function, as B20 first listed: superseded by the class (P2 and C-04).

## Supersedes and sources

Supersedes: none. Sources: owner intent-map item P2 (accepted; mem L401), IAM answer A10 (mem L627), B20 contradiction
(mem L546), all 2026-10-20. Open: owner confirmation that `definePermissions` is not part of the family (C-04).
