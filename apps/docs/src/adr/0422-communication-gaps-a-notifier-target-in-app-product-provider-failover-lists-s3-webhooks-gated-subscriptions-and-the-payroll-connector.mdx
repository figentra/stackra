## Context

The OS notifications and audit services filed gaps: the notifier driver can only reach `messages.message.send@1` with a
mail-only input; an in-app entry has no product; `ProviderResolver` answers one Connection and reads the market only
from context; no generic market-pack `ProviderDefaults` driver ships; webhooks have no S3 target and no entitlement
gate. Payroll needs a connector contract (RV-50: no own payroll).

## Decision

1. **Notifier target.** `NotificationsNotifierDriver` takes a `NotifierTarget`: any action ref and a payload mapper
   that receives the template key, category, principal, market (from a bound `MarketContext`), data and each rendered
   channel, and answers the action's input or `null` to skip. The default stays `messages.message.send@1` with the mail.
2. **In-app product.** `InAppMessage.product` is required, and `InAppStore.list` filters by `product`, for a
   per-product inbox and the ethical wall.
3. **Failover list.** `ProviderResolver#candidates` answers every Connection to try: the tenant's own (mode `own`),
   then the Market's platform ones (mode `managed`), each in pack order. `resolve` is its first entry, loaded lazily.
   Both take the Market as an argument and fall back to `MarketContext`. Whether an own Connection may fail over to a
   managed one is the caller's policy. `ProviderChoice.connectionId` is optional: a bring-your-own-only provider is
   offered with no platform Connection. `MarketPackProviderDefaultsDriver` reads the pack's `providers.<capability>`
   list (live entries, pack order) through the host's `ProviderPackSource`, as payments' driver does.
4. **Webhooks.** An endpoint URL `s3://<bucket>/<prefix>?region=` receives each delivery as an object keyed
   `<prefix>/<type>/<yyyy>/<mm>/<dd>/<eventId>.json`, the signed headers as metadata, written by the `s3`
   `WebhookObjectSink` with the platform role; a tenant's own bucket grants that role by bucket policy, so no tenant
   credential is stored. A registration row may name an entitlement; `register` refuses a gated event without the grant
   and the fan-out rechecks it, through `WebhookEntitlementSource` (fail closed when unbound).
5. **Payroll.** `IntegrationCategory.Payroll` and `PayrollConnector.pushPayInputs(batch, idempotencyKey)` (hours,
   sessions, tips, commission per employee, with the provider's employee id and pay code from the tenant's mapping),
   checked by `assertPayInputs`; `MemoryPayrollConnector` in `./testing`. The ZenHR, Kombo and Gusto connectors and the
   data-transfer export live with the capability's owner and are not decided here.

## Consequences

- A notifications service with another module or input needs no fork of the driver.
- Channel gateways stop repeating the ADR 0356 order rule.
- The audit SIEM stream is gated by plan in one place and can land in a bucket.
