---
title: "0424 — Checklists are workflow-template documents with locked steps, scope and user progress, and industry profiles"
description: "Proposed · 2026-10-26"
---

<Note>
  Proposed (STA-776). It moves the onboarding checklist engine into `@stackra/workflow` as RV-22
  decided (editable templates, Stackra engine) and makes industry templates seed-pack profiles.
</Note>

## Context

Products need onboarding checklists (and later exit and fix lists) that tenants may tailor without losing the steps
the product requires, that track progress for the whole tenant and for each user, and that start differently for a
salon and a restaurant. RV-22 made the templates editable and the engine Stackra's; the seed pack now has a
`checklist-template` record kind and an `industry-template` record kind.

## Decision

1. **A checklist is a seed document.** `ChecklistTemplate$`: key `workflow-template:<product>:<name>` (the onboarding
   one is `workflow-template:<product>:onboarding`), a semantic version and 1 to 50 steps, each a snake_case key, a
   lang-key label and description, a `locked` flag and an optional console route. A scope's copy
   (`workflow.checklists`) moves to a new template version and keeps the scope's customization. A scope may hide or
   reorder unlocked steps only (`ChecklistCustomization$`); a locked step is refused (`step_locked`) and keeps its
   template place.
2. **Progress per scope and per user.** Each step names its audience: `scope` (done once for the tenant or node) or
   `user` (each user does it). `workflow.checklist_progress` keeps one row per checklist, step and owner (the scope id or
   the user id); a step is never un-done.
3. **Completion events.** Completing a step records `ChecklistStepCompleted` once; a completion that leaves every
   visible step done records `ChecklistCompleted` (per user when the checklist has user steps). They are domain events;
   a product relays them when another service needs them.
4. **Industry templates are seed-pack profiles.** An `industry-template` record's checklist part is an
   `IndustryProfile$`: a customization per checklist key, applied at sign-up by `ChecklistService.applyProfile` and
   never over a scope's own customization. Its other parts (settings, rule sets, automations) are seeded by their
   owners.
5. Steps hold no code: completing one is an action the product's own module calls. The console checklist component is
   `@stackra/ui`'s, over `ChecklistService.view`.

## Consequences

- Each product seeds its onboarding checklist as data; a new step is a pack release, not a deploy.
- Locked steps are the product's contract with the tenant: removing one needs a new template version.

## Enforcement
