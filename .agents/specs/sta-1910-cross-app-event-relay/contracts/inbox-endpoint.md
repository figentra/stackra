# Contract: the consumer inbox endpoint

`POST /events/inbox` on a consumer service that listens to at least one contract-only producer's event. Generated by
gen (`.stackra/gen/inbox.ts`, `.stackra/encore/<member>/inbox.ts`); outside session auth, authenticated by signature.

## Request

Headers:

- `webhook-id`: the delivery id (`dlv_…`).
- `webhook-timestamp`: Unix seconds when signed.
- `webhook-signature`: `v1a,<key id>,<base64 Ed25519 signature>` over `<webhook-id>.<webhook-timestamp>.<raw body>`;
  several space-separated values during a rotation.
- `content-type: application/json`.

Body: the cross-app envelope (`id`, `type`, `occurredAt`, `scope`, `meta`, `data`, `envelopeVersion: 1`). A `producer`
field in the body is ignored.

## Verification, in order, before the body is parsed

1. The three headers are present and well formed.
2. The timestamp is within five minutes of the consumer's clock.
3. The key id is listed for the running environment by a producer the consumer pins (its contract's `relay.keys`).
4. The signature verifies with that key.

## Answers

| Status | When                                                                       | Producer does |
| ------ | -------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------- |
| 204    | handled now, already received, or a newer version than the consumer reads  | removes it    |
| 401    | any verification step fails; body never parsed; nothing recorded           | dead-letters  |
| 422    | verified, but the envelope or its data breaks the schema; nothing recorded | dead-letters  |
| 5xx    | a listener threw (the claim rolled back)                                   | retries       |
